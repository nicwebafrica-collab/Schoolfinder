About these fees

Boys' school. Grade R shown. Monthly figure is the annual fee split over the 11 compulsory debit orders (Jan-Nov), calculated by us. Up to 5% off for early payment. Gr 1-2 R105,060, Gr 3 R122,900, Gr 4-6 R148,040, Gr 7 R160,060. The 'deposit' is the non-refundable acceptance fee (R15,500 Gr R-7; R3,700 Gr 0000-00). Early payment 5% by 31 Dec, 4% by 31 Jan, 3% by 28 Feb. The 11 debit orders also include an estimate of incidentals, so the real monthly debit is higher than the fee divided by 11.
