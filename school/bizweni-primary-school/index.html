About these fees

Independent school, Gr R to Gr 7. Entry grade is Gr R (half day): R40,320 a year, R10,080 a term or R3,360 a month for 12 months. Gr 1-7 tuition is R57,360 a year, R14,340 a term or R4,780 a month for 12 months. Gr R pays a R350 non-refundable application fee and R600 stationery (once-off). Gr 1-7 pays a R500 non-refundable application fee and a R3,000 non-refundable registration fee. Gr 1-7 also pays a Resource Fee of R800, listed without saying how often; ask the school. Paying tuition per term or annually in advance earns a 5% discount on tuition only. Monthly payments are due on or before the 3rd, January to December. Sibling discount: 10% off tuition for the second and later children. Aftercare (no meals) is R1,140 a month for 12 months, R3,420 a term or R13,680 a year. Holiday club is R120 a day.
