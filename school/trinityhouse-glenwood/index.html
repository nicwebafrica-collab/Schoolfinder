About these fees

Independent combined school in George (pre-primary, preparatory, high school, with boarding), becoming Trinityhouse Glenwood; 2027 fees. Pay annually before 31 January 2027, or monthly in 12 payments (January to December). The annual price is lower than 12 monthly payments, so it is a pay-in-full price; monthly amounts shown are the 12-payment ones. Gr 12 has only 10 monthly payments (January to October) of R11,830. Non-refundable application fee R600 and enrolment fee R6,000. Pre-primary stationery levy R1,570 and IT & printing levy (Gr 1-12) R3,210, both non-refundable, listed without saying how often; ask the school. Gr 12 IEB exam fees are billed extra by end of March 2027 (no amount printed). Boarding Gr 8-11: weekly R8,700 or termly R12,570 a month (12 payments); boarding placement fee R3,000.
