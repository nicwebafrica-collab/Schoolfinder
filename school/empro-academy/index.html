About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. 2027 fees for Grade R: R57,840 for the year, or R4,820 a month over 12 months. Other grades: Gr 1-3 R60,600 a year or R5,050 a month; Gr 4-7 R65,520 or R5,460; Gr 8-9 R69,600 or R5,800; Gr 10-12 R73,200 or R6,100. The table has no column headings; the labels 'Annual Fee 2027' and 'Monthly over 12 Months' sit beneath it. 'Registration' is the R300 application fee. 'Deposit' is the R3,500 single learner placement fee (non-refundable, per child, paid once the child is accepted); the page doesn't say if it comes off the fees. Siblings enrolling together pay a R5,000 family enrolment fee (non-refundable). Discounts: 8% off total fees if paid in full before 31 January 2027; 8% off school fees for the second and/or third child. Uniforms, textbooks, stationery, camps, outings, photos and special events cost extra (no amounts). A term's notice is needed if a learner leaves.
