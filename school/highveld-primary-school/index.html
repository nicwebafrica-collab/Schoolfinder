About these fees

Public school. 2026 school fee R14,000 per pupil per annum; the schedule does not say which grades it covers. Administration fee R500, one-off, due with the first payment. Four ways to pay: (1) in full by 31 March 2026 with 10% off: R13,100 for the first child, R12,888 for a 2nd sibling, R12,688 for further siblings; (2) in full by 31 January 2026 with 15% early-bird discount: R12,400, R12,200 and R12,011; (3) 12 monthly payments: R1,667 first month, then 11 of R1,167; (4) 10 monthly payments: R1,900 first month, then 9 of R1,400. The discounted prices are as printed and include the R500 admin fee. Exemption forms are available from the finance office after 1 April 2026.
