About these fees

Independent school in Gqeberha. 2027 fees per year (monthly, 10 instalments Jan-Oct): Gr 000 R30,000 (R3,000); Gr PR and R R41,160 (R4,116); Gr 1-3 R54,820 (R5,482); Gr 4-6 R63,480 (R6,348); Gr 7-9 R70,840 (R7,084); Gr 10-11 R81,210 (R8,121); Gr 12 R95,300 (R9,530), which includes IEB exam fees. Registration fee, non-refundable, charged per family when registering together: R2,500 for Gr 000 and PR and R, R4,500 for Gr 1-12. Enrolment fee payable by end February 2027, deductible on progression: R4,100 for Gr R to Gr 1 (for 2028), R5,100 for Gr 7 to Gr 8 (for 2028). Family discount on tuition: 7.5% second child, 12.5% third, 20% fourth. 5% off tuition if paid by 31 January 2027. Development levy R2,862 per year (capped per family) and magazine levy R100 per family. Music fees are listed but not recorded (unclear who pays).
