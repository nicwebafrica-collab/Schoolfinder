About these fees

Fee-paying public girls' primary school. Compulsory school fees for 2026: R25,652 a year per child for Grade 00 to 7, due on the first day of the school year. Options: pay in full by 31 December 2025 and get 10% off (R23,087), pay in full by 31 January 2026 (R23,857), or 11 monthly payments on the last day of the month; ask the school for the monthly amount. The discount applies only to school fees. Optional extras: aftercare (12:30 to 17:15) R700 a month for 10 months, casual day R50; music tuition R320, instrument hire R270 and percussion R60 a month for 10 months; remedial lessons R480 a term.
