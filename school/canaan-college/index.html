About these fees

Independent school. 2027 fees by grade, approved by the school's board; the headline is Grade R. Annual fee R36,040, or monthly R3,005 (the school prints 12 months; 12 x R3,005 is R36,060; in most grades 12 x the monthly amount is a little off the printed annual, so the school's own annual figure is shown). Fees include textbooks and robotics. Paying the whole year by 31 January 2027 saves 5%. Sibling discounts: 5% for the 2nd child, 7.5% for the 3rd and more; 15% for new siblings in the primary school only. Registration fee R2,000, not refunded, paid on acceptance.
