About these fees

Independent school from pre-primary to Gr 12. Each grade has a discounted annual price (Gr 0000 R61,188) that is lower than 12 monthly payments added up, so the monthly figure shown is the normal 12-payment option (Gr 0000 R5,470; Gr 1 R8,010; Gr 8 R12,250). Gr 12 has only a 10-payment option, R18,800 a month. The discount is lost if not paid in full by 31 January 2027. New learners pay a R880 application fee and a R5,500 non-refundable enrolment fee. Pre-primary aftercare (Crawford Kidz Club) full day is R1,500 a month on the 12-payment option, or R250 a day ad hoc. 10-payment amounts and one unlabelled row of R15,720, R1,670 and R1,500 are left out because the sheet text was not clear.
