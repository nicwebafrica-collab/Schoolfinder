About these fees

2027 day fees, Grade R shown (Gr 11-12 R191,280). Discount if paid in full by 31 Jan; otherwise monthly over 10 months (Jan-Oct). Compulsory variable billings come on top, per grade (see extras). The 'deposit' is the Capital Development & Enrolment Fee (R22,680 in 2027). Application fee R800 (website also says R750). One full term's notice to leave. Grade 9 carries the heaviest 'variable billing' of the whole school, because The Journey is folded into it.
