About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12, with an early childhood centre. The 2026 sheet gives three ways to pay tuition: monthly, termly (3% discount) or yearly before 31 January 2026 (6% discount). Gr R-1: R2,750 a month, R8,003 a term or R31,020 a year; Gr 2-7: R2,850, R8,294 or R32,148; Gr 8: R3,424, R9,963 or R38,623; Gr 12: R4,730, R13,765 or R53,354 (other grades in the grade list). Early childhood RR and RRR programmes are listed separately (full day R2,950 a month). Registration fee: R1,000 for the early childhood centre and preparatory school, R800 for high school. A yearly levy (R2,100 Gr R-7, R2,150 Gr 8-11) is due by 31 January; discounts do not apply to it. The sheet prints the RRR half-day yearly fee (R34,968) higher than full day (R33,276). Sibling discount is 5% per sibling. Late payments attract interest at 18.5%.
