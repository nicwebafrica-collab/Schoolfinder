About these fees

Independent school, Pre-Prep to Grade 12. Fees are by phase; the headline is the Pre-Prep (Gr 000-0) monthly fee of R6,360 for 12 payments, January to December. The school also prints a lower annual price for payment before 31 January 2027 (R69,972 for Pre-Prep); 12 monthly payments cost more in total, so we show no yearly figure. Gr 12 pays R14,530 for 10 payments, January to October, and includes IEB exam fees. Gr 1-3 R8,540 a month, Gr 4-6 R9,280, Gr 7 R10,530, Gr 8-9 R12,030, Gr 10-11 R12,570. Application fee R650 and enrolment fee R6,000 are non-refundable. Optional aftercare, holiday care and music are extra. Late collection after 17h30 costs R250 per 15 minutes.
