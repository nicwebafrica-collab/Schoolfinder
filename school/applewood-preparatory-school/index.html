About these fees

Independent school, Apple Blossoms to Grade 7. 2026 fees: Apple Blossoms R40,590 a year or R3,690 a month for 11 months (January to November); fees rise by grade up to R91,300 a year for Grades 4 to 7 (see grade list). Part-week places for Apple Blossoms: 2 days R22,990, 3 days R27,830, 4 days R33,000 a year. Annual payment by 31 January gets a 2.5% discount on school fees. R300 application fee, non-refundable, paid with the application. R3,000 non-refundable acceptance fee per child enrolled, which the sheet doesn't say comes off the fees. A swimming pool levy of R180 per child per month is not included in the fees; the sheet doesn't say for how many months or who pays, so ask the school.
