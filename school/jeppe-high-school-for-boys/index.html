About these fees

Fee-paying public boys' high school with boarding. 2026 school fees R71,200 a year: pay R68,700 once-off by 31 January 2026 (or R67,700 by 31 December 2025), or 4 termly payments of R17,800, or monthly. Grades 8-11 pay R7,120 x 10 months (January-October); Grade 12 pays R8,900 x 8 months (January-August). New boys (all Grade 8s and new Grade 9-11s) pay R6,500 in advance, taken off the first month's fees, and are asked for a voluntary, non-refundable R6,500 development levy. Boarding is billed separately: R81,800 a year for weekly boarders, R90,200 for termly boarders, plus a R3,000 mattress levy for new boarders. These are 2026 fees; the 2027 sheet is not out yet.
