About these fees

Public school. 2026 school fees from the school's letter of 10 December 2025: Gr R R5,050 a year; Gr 1-7 R2,300 a year. The registration payment (R1,050 for Gr R, R500 for Gr 1-7, due from all learners before the end of March 2026) is part of the fee, not an extra. The rest is paid in 10 monthly payments as printed: R400 for Gr R, R180 for Gr 1-7. Applications for school fee exemption open in January 2026.
