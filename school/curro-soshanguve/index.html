About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. 2027 school fees are paid over 12 months, January to December. Grade R is R3,600 a month (R43,200 for the year); Gr 1 R3,790; Gr 2 R3,810; Gr 3 R4,115; Gr 4 R4,510; Gr 5-7 R4,540; Gr 8 R4,830; Gr 9 R4,840; Gr 10 R5,080; Gr 11 R5,110; Gr 12 R5,140 a month (see the grade list). 'Registration' is the non-refundable R2,500 enrolment fee, payable on approval of the application (same for primary and high). Primary compulsory PM YF is R130 a month. Optional return bus R1,710 a month (R20,520 for the year; bus fees may change). 5% off school fees if paid for any 12 months in advance, 2.5% off if paid for any 6 months in advance, and up to 10% for siblings from the third child. Debit orders are scheduled monthly in advance for 12 months.
