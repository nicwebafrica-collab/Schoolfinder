About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to 12. 2026 annual fees by grade: Gr R R45,045; Gr 1-3 R69,564; Gr 4-6 R79,079; Gr 7-9 R86,757; Gr 10-12 R94,138. Monthly over 11 months: R4,095, R6,324, R7,189, R7,887 and R8,558 respectively. Pay the full year by 31 January for 7.5% off (Gr R R41,666.63; Gr 1-3 R64,346.70; Gr 4-6 R73,148.08; Gr 7-9 R80,250.23; Gr 10-12 R87,077.65). Fees paid per term in advance get a 4.5% discount. An annual levy of R1,980 (or R180 a month for 11 months) is invoiced to every learner. Grade 10-12 pay IEB exam fees of R3,495 for 2026 (spread over three years, rising about R100 a year); the sheet doesn't say how often it is billed; ask the school. Fees do not include excursions, workbooks and photographs. Payments after the 7th of the month attract a R250 late fee per learner.
