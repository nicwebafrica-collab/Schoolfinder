About these fees

Independent combined school, Grade 1 to Grade 10. 2027 fee schedule: 'Monthly Fee (12 Months)' is R2,400 for Grades 1 to 6, R2,850 for Grades 7 to 9 and R3,150 for Grade 10. A registration fee of R2,600 is charged for every grade, payable on registration for new and existing learners, and is non-refundable. Monthly fees are paid in advance by EFT, cash or debit order. Cash deposits into the school's account are not allowed and carry a R200 penalty. The school prints no yearly total, so we show none. Fees cover tuition and curriculum; parents buy textbooks and stationery (no amounts given). No early-payment discount is listed.
