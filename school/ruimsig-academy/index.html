About these fees

Independent school; sheet covers Gr 0000 to Gr 12. 2027 tuition per year (monthly over 12 payments): Gr 0000 R45,600 (R3,800); Gr 000-00 R49,200 (R4,100); Gr R R51,600 (R4,300); Gr 1-3 R60,600 (R5,050); Gr 4-6 R66,000 (R5,500); Gr 7 R75,600 (R6,300); Gr 8-9 R75,600 (R6,300); Gr 10 R80,400 (R6,700); Gr 11-12 R87,600 (R7,300). Exam fees are not included. Once-off application fee R800 (Gr 0000 to Gr 11) and once-off, non-refundable registration fee R4,800. A Development Fund Fee of R1,950 is printed against every grade as payable in November 2026; the sheet does not say how often it is charged. Family discount: 5% for a second child, 10% for a third or later child. Upfront-payment discounts on 2027 tuition: 5% if paid before 30 November 2026, 2.5% before 31 December 2026.
