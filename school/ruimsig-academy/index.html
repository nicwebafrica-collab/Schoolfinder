About these fees

Independent school, Gr 0000 to Gr 11. 2026 tuition per year (monthly over 12 instalments): Gr 0000-000 R45,600 (R3,800); Gr 00 R48,300 (R4,025); Gr R R51,600 (R4,300); Gr 1-3 R57,600 (R4,800); Gr 4-5 R61,200 (R5,100); Gr 6 R66,000 (R5,500); Gr 7 R70,800 (R5,900); Gr 8-9 R73,080 (R6,090); Gr 10-11 R77,160 (R6,430). Exam fees are not included. Once-off application fee R800 and once-off, non-refundable registration fee R4,800. Development fee R1,950 a year, printed against every grade. Family discount: 5% for a second child, 10% for a third or later child. Early-payment discounts on the sheet (5% before 30 November 2025, 2.5% before 31 December 2025) have passed. Bus and aftercare fees are separate; bus is billed for 10 months.
