About these fees

Independent Waldorf school, Playgroup to Gr 13 (Matric). 2027 fees. Playgroup and Kindergarten (entry grade shown): annual tuition R63,053 plus annual sundry and levy fees R7,368, or R5,868 a month paid by the 10th over 12 months. The sheet prints no single annual total, so no yearly figure is shown; tuition and sundry fees are shown separately per grade. Other grades range from R7,864 a month (Gr 3) to R10,365 a month (Gr 13). Paying annually earns a 4% discount (before the first day of Term 1) or 2.5% (by 10 February), calculated on tuition only; there is also a termly option. Building levy (R355 first child, R175 each later child) is on the sheet but it does not say how often it is charged, so it is left out. Registration and application fees are not on the sheet.
