About these fees

Independent school, Grade 7 to Grade 12, plus post-matric subjects at R4,050 per subject. Fees are paid monthly in advance by debit order over 12 months, or as one annual fee. 5% off if paid in full before 31 January 2027. Non-refundable registration fee of R5,400 per learner on acceptance; the registration fee and the first month's fee must be paid to secure a place. Late payment fee R500 and returned debit order fee R150. The school says the schedule is valid from 1 March 2026 to 1 March 2028 and may change without notice.
