About these fees

Independent boys' school from Grade 00 to Grade 12. 2026 tuition: Gr 00 R56,920, Gr R R85,000, Gr 1 R122,040, Gr 2-3 R133,840, Gr 4-7 R150,120, Gr 8-12 R183,520. On top of tuition each grade pays a development levy, a book charge and accident insurance (amounts differ by grade), plus a voluntary levy (R5,380 Gr 00, R5,700 other grades). 'Deposit' is the once-off acceptance fee, which rises with the grade (R4,500 for Gr 00 up to R11,500 for Gr 8-12). Gr 12 pays a R9,760 IEB exam fee. Weekly boarding for Gr 8-12 is R129,800 a year. A 33% tuition discount applies to the third and later brothers at the school at the same time. The school's page lists the amounts without saying 'per year' or how to pay; they are shown here as yearly amounts (boarding is listed as R129,800 a year or R32,450 a term).
