About these fees

Public primary school (Afrikaans fee sheet) with a Naskool (after-school care). 2026 school fees are paid in equal instalments from January to November 2026. Grade R: R39,215 a year (printed as 'R39 215.00.00') or R3,565 a month; Grade 1-6: R32,890 a year or R2,990 a month; Grade 7: R33,440 a year or R3,040 a month. Pay in full by 28 February 2026 for a once-off discount: Gr R R1,960.75 (so R37,254.25), Gr 1-6 R1,644.50 (R31,245.50), Gr 7 R1,672 (R31,768). There is 2.5% off for debit order accounts, credited monthly, and 5% off school fees for families with 3 or more learners. Naskool is optional, by debit order only: R15,290 a year or R1,390 a month, plus a R150 Naskool registration fee.
