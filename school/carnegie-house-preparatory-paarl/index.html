About these fees

Independent school, babies (The Nest) to Grade 7; this is the Paarl fee sheet. 2026 tuition has four payment options per class: monthly by EFT, monthly by debit order (3% less), termly (5% discount) or annual (7.5% discount); all are in the grade list. For example Grade R, 5 days, is R3,960 a month by EFT. Junior House (Gr 1-3) is R5,110 and Senior House (Gr 4-7) R5,660 a month by EFT. Once-off on entry: R500 application fee per family; refundable deposit R4,000 per child (preschool) or R5,500 (primary); non-refundable development fee R1,500. Sheet totals: R6,000 preschool, R7,500 primary. Annual levy: preschool R1,200, primary R2,100 (also payable termly or monthly). Second and third child get 10% off school fees. Aftercare amounts are on the sheet but how often they are charged is unclear; ask the school.
