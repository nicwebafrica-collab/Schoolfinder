About these fees

Public Afrikaans-medium primary school, Gr R to Gr 7. 2027 school fees per learner per year: Gr R to Gr 7 R32,615; the Curriculum Integration (SKI) class for Gr 4, 5 and 6 R49,940. Monthly: Gr R to Gr 6 R2,965 over 11 months; Gr 7 R3,261.50 over 10 months; SKI class R4,540 a month. Paying in full on or before 26 February 2027 gives R955 off (R31,660, or R48,985 for the SKI class). Optional extras: tennis, chess and drama are charged per quarter (see extras); sport levy is R100 per code capped at R500 and culture levy R100 per code, but the page does not say how often, so they are left out. Gr 6 and Gr 7 educational tours are estimates only and are left out. A R3,100 advance is asked from Gr R parents for the following year. Aftercare R1,230 a month for registered learners.
