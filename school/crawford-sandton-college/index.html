About these fees

Independent college, Grade 8 to 12. 2027 tuition is printed three ways: an annual (discounted) price if paid in full by 31 January 2027, or 12 monthly payments, or 10 monthly payments. The annual price is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so only the 12-month monthly amount is shown. Grade 12 has no 12-month option, only 10 payments. Application fee R880 and non-refundable enrolment fee R13,200 are for new learners, paid through the school's online portal. Aftercare does not apply to the College. Music lessons are listed, but the sheet's columns could not be matched reliably, so no music amounts are shown; ask the school.
