About these fees

Independent preparatory school, Gr 1-7. Each grade has a discounted annual price (Gr 1 R133,236) that is lower than 12 monthly payments added up, so the monthly figure shown is the normal 12-payment option (Gr 1 R11,920 a month). A 10-payment option is also printed (Gr 1 R14,020). The discount is lost if not paid in full by 31 January 2027. New learners pay a R880 application fee and a R13,200 non-refundable enrolment fee, both through the school's online portal. The sheet also lists Full Day, Half Day and Option rows that it does not label, so they are left out; ask the school about aftercare and music.
