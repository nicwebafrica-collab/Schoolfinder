About these fees

2027 fees (Grade R shown). Paid over 12 months, or 5% off if paid in full by 31 Jan. Sibling discount 5% / 10% / 15% for 2nd / 3rd / 4th child, must be requested by email. Stationery, workbooks and textbooks included for Gr R-3; unclear for Gr 4-7 (parents supply stationery). Withdrawal needs a full term's notice. 2026 Grade R was R57,250. Twice on the journey (February of Grade 00 and of Grade 7) the school asks for R3,600 as a 'promise to stay'; it melts into the next January's account, but stays behind with the school if you leave.
