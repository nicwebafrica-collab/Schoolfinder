About these fees

Public school, Grade R to 7. 2026 annual fee: Grade R R40,000; Grades 1-7 R33,500 (the page labels the annual fee 'once off'). All fees are payable in advance over 10 months, January to October: R4,000 a month for Grade R, R3,350 a month for Grades 1-7. A 10% discount is given if fees are paid in full on or before 31 December for the next year, and 5% if paid in full by 31 January for the current year; the page prints percentages only. The page lists no other fees; ask the school about levies and extras.
