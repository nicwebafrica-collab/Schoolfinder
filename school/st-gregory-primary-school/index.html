About these fees

Independent school, pre-school to Gr 7, in Estcourt. Fees shown are for Pre-school Gr RRR & RR: R21,750 a year, or 4 quarterly payments of R5,437.50, or R1,980 a month over 11 months (15 January to 15 November 2027). Fees differ by grade (see grade list). The school says its fees include education, sport fixtures, excursions, art supplies, 2027 textbooks and learner accident insurance. Discounts: 5% off all fees if paid in full by the end of February; sibling discounts on full fees of 5% for the 2nd child, 7.5% for the 3rd and 10% for the 4th. Fees are due in advance, at the start of each month or term. Cash is not accepted at the office. The sheet lists no registration, deposit or other levies.
