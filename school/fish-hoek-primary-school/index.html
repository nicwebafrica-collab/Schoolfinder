About these fees

Fee-charging public primary school, Pre Gr R to Gr 7. 2027 fees for Pre Gr R and Gr R are R41,730 a year, or R4,173 a month for 10 months (1 February to 1 November). Gr 1-7 is R36,500 a year, or R3,650 a month for 10 months. Paying in full by the end of February gives a discount: Pre Gr R and Gr R R2,086.50 (R39,643.50 to pay); Gr 1-7 with 1 child R1,825 (R34,675), 2 children R3,650 (R69,350), 3 children R5,475 (R104,025). Aftercare, listed 'over 11 months (1 Jan - 1 Nov)' and including lunch, is R1,616 (13h00-18h00), R1,380 (14h30-18h00) or R1,129 (13h00-16h00); the sheet doesn't say how often these are charged, so ask the school. Holiday care is R180 for a full day and R130 for a half day (5.5 hours), also without a stated frequency. Optional music is R658 a month half time or R1,065 a month full time. No registration fee is listed.
