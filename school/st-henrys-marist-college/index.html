About these fees

Independent school, Little Lions to Gr 12; boarding for boys in Gr 7 to 12. Fees shown are for Little Lions (also Gr 000, Gr 00 and Gr R): R61,960 a year, or 4 termly payments of R15,490, or R6,196 for 10 months. Monthly options over 11 or 12 months are debit order only and interest bearing, so no single monthly figure is shown. Fees differ by grade (see grade list), up to R161,890 for Gr 10 to 12. A Capital Development Levy is charged per family: R4,000 for one child, R6,000 for more than one child (lump sum or over ten months). Discounts (school fees only, one type per student): 6% if the full annual fee is paid by 31 December 2026, 5% by 31 January 2027; 5% sibling discount for the 2nd and further children. Interest of 2% per month applies to overdue accounts. Notice period is one term. The sheet lists no registration or deposit.
