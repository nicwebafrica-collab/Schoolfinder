About these fees

Public high school. The school's annual fee for 2026 is R37,800 per learner, the same for every learner as printed. Pay annually in advance and get a 5% discount if paid by the end of February (the school doesn't print the rand amount), or pay monthly in advance over ten months, from 1 February to 1 November (no monthly amount is printed). EFT is preferred; the Bursar's office also accepts credit cards. Fee exemptions are available; the application form comes from the Finance Department in February. The page lists no other fees.
