About these fees

2026 fees, Class 1 (Grade 1) shown, first child. Waldorf 'Class N' is Grade N; Class 13 is an optional year after matric. Paid monthly over 12 months (yearly = 12 x monthly). 2nd and 3rd child pay less (e.g. Class 1 R4,802, Class 12 R8,537 a month). The R5,000 'registration' is a non-refundable placement fee. 6.5% off if paid yearly by 31 Dec 2025, 5% by 20 Jan 2026, 4% for half a year in advance. Fees read from the school's online 2026 fee calculator (screenshots, 27 Oct 2026). Pre-school extras (class fund R150, insurance R135, paper levy) are listed but only Grade 1-12 totals include them. The R100 building levy is counted per household, not per child, so a second sibling doesn't double it.
