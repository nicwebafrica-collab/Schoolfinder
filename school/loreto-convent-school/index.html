About these fees

Independent school. 2027 fees for Gr RRR and RR (aftercare included): R51,480 a year or R4,680 a month for 11 months (January to November). Fees differ by grade, up to R78,540 a year in Gr 10-12; Gr 12 is printed as R11,220 for 7 months. 2.5% discount if settled before 28 February 2027 (Gr RRR-RR R1,287). Each grade from Gr R also has a once-off annual extra fee, and every account pays a R850 building levy in January 2027. Re-registration fee R6,500, due by 31 October 2026 and allocated to January 2027 fees. Optional aftercare from Gr R: R4,500 a year or R450 a month from February to November. 5% sibling discount on school fees for each sibling except the oldest (not Gr RRR-RR).
