About these fees

Public school, Gr 4 to Gr 7. The admissions page prints one 2026 fee for Gr 4-7: R32,500 per pupil per annum, or R3,250 a month over 10 months (January to October). Fees are payable in advance and any arrangements are made with the Bursar. The page offers a discount (currently 5%) for paying the full annual fee by the end of February; for 2026 that date has passed. Learners placed in the Q Class pay no additional school fee levies. The page prints no registration or application fee, and no aftercare, transport or outing costs.
