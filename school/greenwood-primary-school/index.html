About these fees

Public school, Gr R to Gr 7. 2026 school fees are compulsory: R2,170 a month over 11 instalments (January to November), R5,967.50 a term, or R23,870 a year. One fee for all grades. A 10% discount on the school fee only for paying the full year in advance (by 28 February 2026) has passed. Optional extras: music tuition and instrument rental, and aftercare (monthly payment is not an option for aftercare; termly or annual only). The sheet does not say whether the extras qualify for the discount.
