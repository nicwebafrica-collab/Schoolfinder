About these fees

Grade 1-4 shown (includes book levy). 5% off if paid in full by 31 Jan; quarterly option. Sibling rebate 5% (2nd), 15% (3rd), 25% (4th+). Deposit is 15% of the year's fees on acceptance (about R15,786 for Gr 1-4). Fees are higher if a company pays. Deposit shown is 15% of a year's fees for Gr 1-4 (refundable). Registration shown = R880 application fee + R1,100 admission fee (both non-refundable, new learners).
