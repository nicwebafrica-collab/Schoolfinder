About these fees

2027 fees, Grade 1-4 shown (includes the school book levy). 5% off if paid in full by 31 Jan 2027; quarterly option. Sibling rebate 5% (2nd), 15% (3rd), 25% (4th+). Fees are higher if a company or other third party pays (e.g. Gr 1-4 R144,360). Registration shown = R930 application fee + R1,160 admission fee (both non-refundable, new learners). Deposit shown = 15% of the year's fees on acceptance (R16,650 for Gr 1-4, our calculation; refundable, interest-free). Learners need medical aid or insurance.
