About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to Grade 12, 2027 fees. Tuition per year: RR and R R91 740, Gr 1-6 R103 620, Gr 7 R120 560, Gr 8-12 R127 600. Or monthly for 11 months: R8 340, R9 420, R10 960 and R11 600 (a small sibling discount applies to monthly payers). Paying the year before 31 March: R88 070, R99 470, R115 738 and R122 496. New learners pay an application fee of R580 and a non-refundable enrolment fee of R7 720 on acceptance. The R3 000 development levy is not payable in the first year. The school says tuition includes photographs, outings, software licences and sport transport. Book levy, stationery fee, textbooks, Chromebook (Gr 6+) and excursions are listed without amounts; ask the school. Drama is R695 to R1 130 and Orff R670 to R960 a term by grade; aftercare to 15h00 and club grades are unclear on the flyer.
