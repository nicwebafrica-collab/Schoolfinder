About these fees

Public primary school in Blanco, George. 2026 school fees (Skoolfooie vir 2026), paid monthly over 11 months or per year: Bokkieland R27,500 a year (R2,500 a month); Gr RR and Gr R R16,940 (R1,540); Gr 1 to Gr 7 R16,060 (R1,460). Headline shows Bokkieland, the lowest class listed. Once-off registration fee R250, new learners only. Gr RR and Gr R pay a once-off R1,540 loom (stationery and support materials) fee with registration. Aftercare (Nasorgsentrum, Gr RR to Gr 7) is R710 a month for the first child (second child R610, third child R510), or R50 a day; lunch packs R60 a day if needed.
