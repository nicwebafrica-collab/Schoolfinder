About these fees

Public primary school in Blanco, George. 2027 school fees (Skoolfooie vir 2027), paid monthly over 11 months or per year, by the sheet's own grade labels: Gr RRR (2 to 4 years) R28,600 a year (R2,600 a month); Gr RR (5 years) R17,600 (R1,600); Gr R (6 years) R17,600 (R1,600); Gr 1 to Gr 7 R16,940 (R1,540). Headline shows Gr RRR, the lowest class listed. Once-off registration fee R250, new learners only (printed with the Gr 1 to Gr 7 fee). Gr RR and Gr R pay a once-off R1,600 LOOM fee (stationery and support materials) with registration. Gr RRR also has a LOOM fee of R2,600, payable by the end of November; the sheet does not say how often it is charged, so it is not listed as a cost. Aftercare (Nasorgsentrum, Gr RR to Gr 7) is R760 a month for the first child, or R54 a day; meal packs R65 a day per child if needed.
