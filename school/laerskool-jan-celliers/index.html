About these fees

Independent school, Baby school to Gr 7 (sheet in Afrikaans). 2026 school fees per year: Baby and Play school R76,000; Gr 000 and 00 R82,000; Gr 0 R89,000; Gr 1 R105,000; Gr 2 and 3 R124,000; Gr 4 to 7 R138,000. Fees are paid over 11 months, January to October, with a monthly amount (Gr 1 R9,545) or a lower early-payment amount (Gr 1 R98,000, by 30 November 2025, already past). The monthly amounts do not add up exactly to the yearly fee, so no monthly figure is shown here. Books and outings are included in the school fund. Registration is a once-off, non-refundable R3,000 on signing the contract. Optional extras are listed below. After-care per afternoon (R325 primary, R165 Baby/Play) is listed without saying how often; ask the school. No 2027 fees on the sheet.
