About these fees

Independent primary school, Gr 1 to Gr 7. Tuition is paid monthly in advance for 12 months: Gr 1-3 R1,700 a month, R20,400 a year; Gr 4-7 R1,800 a month, R21,600 a year (excluding registration). Registration is a once-off, compulsory, non-refundable R900 for 2026 Grade 1 pupils and new learners. Learners from other schools in Gr 1-7 pay a R450 assessment test fee. Compulsory once-off stationery and workbooks: Gr 1-3 R2,000 (R750 stationery, R1,250 workbooks); Gr 4-7 R2,300 (stationery and textbooks). Aftercare R550 a month. A R150 late payment penalty (late fetching of learners is listed together with it). Transport is available in some areas and charged separately.
