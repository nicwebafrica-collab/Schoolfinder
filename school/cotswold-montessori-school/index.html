About these fees

Independent Montessori school with preschool and primary (Year 1-7). The fee sheet is headed 'Fee Schedule 2026'. Monthly tuition, paid over 12 months (January to December) in advance on the 1st: 2 to 4 year olds R4,035; 5 year olds R4,100; Pre-primary R4,800; Year 1-3 R6,050; Year 4-7 R6,300. Fees shown are for 2 to 4 year olds. A non-refundable R4,000 enrolment fee is paid (shown here as the deposit). Pay the whole year before the end of February for a 5% discount; each pupil after the oldest gets 6% off the listed fees. Early Morning Class, Waiting Class and Aftercare are also billed as a monthly amount for 11 months (R125, R580, R900); that is not listed as a yearly amount, so ask the school. Cash is not accepted.
