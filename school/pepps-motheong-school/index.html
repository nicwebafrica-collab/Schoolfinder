About these fees

Independent school, Gr R to Gr 12. 2026 tuition in 11 monthly instalments: Gr R-9 R3,500; Gr 10-11 R3,755; Gr 12 R4,082. The yearly figures here are 11 x the monthly amount (R38,500, R41,305, R44,902); the page prints annual totals that include the R2,500 registration fee (R41,000, R43,805, R47,402). The page calls the R2,500 a once-off registration fee, while the linked 2026 PDF calls it re-registration, so who pays it is not certain. Sibling discounts per month: 2nd child R3,360 / R3,605 / R3,919, 3rd child R3,226 / R3,461 / R3,762. The 2.5% discount for paying in full before 31 January 2026 has passed. FET textbook fee R900 a year for Gr 10-12. Excursions, extra activities, stationery and workbooks are separate. 2027 fees are not published.
