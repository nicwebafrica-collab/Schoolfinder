About these fees

Public high school with hostels. 2026 school fees per learner R42,960 (the sheet does not say 'per year' but prints R10,740 per term, paid in advance, or 10 equal monthly payments of R4,296, paid in advance). Pay in full by 28 February 2026 for 10% off: R38,664. New pupils pay a R4,400 new pupil fee in advance; it forms part of the 2026 fees. Hostel fees exclude school fees. Weekly boarders: R68,970, new pupil fee R18,000 (part of the 2026 fees), terms of R20,691, R20,691, R13,794 and R13,794, or R6,897 a month for 10 months by debit order. Termly boarders: R84,645, new pupil fee R18,000, terms of R25,393.50, R25,393.50, R16,929 and R16,929, or R8,464.50 a month for 10 months by debit order. The hostel fees are listed without saying how often; ask the school.
