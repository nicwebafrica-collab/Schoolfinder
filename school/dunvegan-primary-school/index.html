About these fees

Public school charging fees. 2026 fees are R24,816 per pupil per year. Payment options as printed: monthly manual payments (including EFT) over 11 months, R2,256 a month; full settlement due 31 January 2026, R23,079 a year; debit order over 11 months, R2,143.18 a month (R23,575 a year). Because several monthly options are printed, no single monthly figure is shown. Penalty: if any payment is missed, the full R24,816 becomes due; the page does not say how often or when this is charged. Fees are payable in full unless a fee exemption is applied for. No registration fee or other levies are listed.
