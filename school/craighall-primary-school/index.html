About these fees

Fee-paying public primary school. 2026 school fee per learner: Grade R R51,000 (or R5,100 a month for 10 months); Grade 4-7 R39,000 (or R3,900 a month for 10 months). On top of the school fee the sheet adds a levy (Grade R R2,000, Grade 4-7 R1,200) and a book fee (Grade R R1,500, Grade 4-7 R1,300). Grade R also shows a R8,600 deposit, which is taken off the amount payable, so it is part of the year's fees, not extra. Discounts on the school fee: 8% if paid in full by 31 December 2025, 2% by 31 January 2026. The sheet also has tables for Grades 1-3, but their grade labels could not be read from the scanned sheet, so ask the school for Grade 1-3 fees.
