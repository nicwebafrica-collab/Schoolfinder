About these fees

Independent school; the sheet does not list the grades. 2027 school fees R97,200 a year, with three payment options: 12 monthly payments of R8,100 (January to December), 10 monthly payments of R9,720 (January to October), or once-off by 31 October 2026 with a 15% discount, R82,620. Payments are due in advance by the 1st of each month. Registration deposit R16,200, payable on acceptance. Sibling discount: the sheet gives both 10% of the monthly fee and R7,290 a month; ask the school. Optional aftercare until 15:15 is R1,300 a month and until 17:30 R1,800 a month, both January to November; ad hoc aftercare R165 per day. Books for Gr 1-3 and the Learner Support Programme R1,800; Gr 4-12 buy their own.
