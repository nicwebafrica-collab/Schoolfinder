About these fees

Public school, Gr 4-7 (grades from the school website; the fee sheet does not print them). 2026 tuition is R18,000 per child a year, payable in advance: R1,800 a month in 10 instalments by the 7th of each month from January to October, or R4,500 a term in 4 instalments within the first 10 days of each term. A 5% discount (R17,100) applied to a full year paid by 28 February 2026; that date has passed. There is also an Additional Resource Fee of R100 per learner; the sheet calls it once-off and an annual fee, so it is shown as a yearly cost. Registration and application fees are not on the sheet. Fee reduction can be applied for through the governing body. No general office email is published.
