About these fees

Independent school, Gr 1 to Gr 12. 2027 tuition by grade: Gr 1-3 11 x R2300 (R25 300 per year), Gr 4-7 11 x R2400 (R26 400 per year), Gr 8-11 11 x R2500 (R27 500 per year), Gr 12 11 x R2600 (R28 600 per year). Fees are paid in 11 payments, January to November, up front at the start of each month and before the 15th; the school says payment in instalments is allowed. 'Registration' is the R400 application fee for new students (non-refundable, paid with the application). After an acceptance letter a holding fee (deposit) equal to one month's fee must be paid; it is deducted from the January fees. The school prints no rand figure for it, so none is shown. Current learners pay a R200 re-registration fee (non-refundable). The sheet lists no other levies and no discounts.
