About these fees

Public Afrikaans-medium primary school, Gr RR to Gr 7. 2026 school fees per year (monthly over 11 instalments, January to November): Gr RR R34,100 (R3,100); Gr R R32,450 (R2,950); Gr 1 R28,050 (R2,550); Gr 2-7 R25,850 (R2,350). The page also lists a once-off price for payment before 28 February with 7,5% off (Gr RR R31,543, Gr R R30,016, Gr 1 R25,946, Gr 2-7 R23,911); that date has passed for the 2026 year. Fees were set at the annual general meeting of 5 November 2025. Fee-reduction (subsidy) applications are handled by the governing body. No deposit or registration fee is mentioned on the notice.
