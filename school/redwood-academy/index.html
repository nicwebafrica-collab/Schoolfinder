About these fees

Independent school; the 2027 prospectuses cover Grade RRR to Grade 3. Monthly fee is R 2 950 per month in the ECD prospectus (Grade RRR to R) and R 5 100 per month in the Foundation Phase prospectus (Grade 1-3). The school prints no yearly total or number of months. Registration is listed as its own item: new applicants R 2 800; current learners R 2 000. The prospectus also prints 'payable over 12 months' (new applicants) and 'payable over 11 months' (current learners), but it is not clear whether this refers to the registration or the monthly fee; ask the school. Shown here is the new-applicant amount. After care is R 950 per month (optional). Madressah Islamic Studies is optional at an extra cost, no amount printed. Coding, robotics, swimming, soccer and netball are listed as free.
