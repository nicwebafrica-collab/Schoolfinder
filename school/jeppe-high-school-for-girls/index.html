About these fees

State-aided school, Gr 8-12. 2027 school fee is one amount for all grades: R58,000. It is payable over ten months from the beginning of January to the end of October; Gr 12 is payable over eight months, January to August. A monthly amount is not printed. Discounts printed: 5% (R2,900) for full upfront annual payment by 31 December 2026 and 4% (R2,320) by 31 January 2027. A debit-order discount is also printed (2%, 'to the value of R 980') but the percentage and rand value do not agree, so it is left out. Weekly boarding is R89,000 for 2027. No registration, application or other fees are listed on the page.
