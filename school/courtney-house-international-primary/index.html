About these fees

Independent primary phase, Reception to Year 6. 2027 tuition for Reception: R10,100 a month, R121,200 for the year, payable over 12 months; Year 1 is R12,500 a month and Years 4 to 6 are R13,520 a month. A non-refundable enrolment fee of R8,925 is payable when the application is approved. Transport is extra: one-way R2,625 a month, return R3,490 a month (subject to change). Cambridge Primary Checkpoint (Year 6) is R1,620 per subject. Savings: 5% on school fees for paying 12 months in advance, 2.5% for 6 months in advance, up to 5% for siblings from the second child. Textbooks, stationery, photos, excursions and uniforms are extra.
