About these fees

Independent school: Junior School Grades 4-7, Senior School Grades 8-12 (IEB). 2026 tuition by grade; the headline is the Junior School (Gr 4-7). Total fees are tuition plus levies; levies charged to all students are IEB, Food Day, Social Action and Grade Camp (plus a Prep excursions levy in Gr 4-7). Subject levies (art, isiXhosa camp, CAT, design, drama, science camps) apply only where a student takes the subject. Pay the total in 12 equal monthly instalments (1 January to 1 December 2026), or in full by 31 January 2026 for 5% off the tuition fee. Sibling discount 5% of tuition. Three months' written notice to leave. These are 2026 fees; the 2027 schedule is not out yet.
