About these fees

Independent school: Junior School Grades 4-7, Senior School Grades 8-12 (IEB). 2027 tuition (base fee) per year: Gr 4-7 R106,800; Gr 8-9 R142,715; Gr 10-12 R147,100. The headline is Gr 4-7. Total fees are tuition plus levies. The letter says the IEB Examination, Food Day, Grade Camps and Social Action levies are charged to all students, the Prep levy to Prep School students, and subject levies only where a student takes the subject. The levy columns are misaligned in the text, so no levy amounts are shown here; see the school's letter. Payment options: full settlement discount on the base fee of 10% by 30 November, 7.5% by 31 December, 5% by 31 January; or three terms (50% / 25% / 25%); or 8 monthly payments January to August; or a 12-month plan with a R2 500 surcharge. The letter is dated 23 October 2026.
