About these fees

Independent combined school, Grade R to Grade 12. 2027 school fees for Grade R: R4,120 a month, R49,440 for the year, payable over 12 months (January to December); fees rise by grade (Grade 7 R4,820 a month, Grade 12 R5,950 a month). A non-refundable enrolment fee is payable when the application is approved; its amount is not printed. The letters also list a R3,300 amount under a 'School Fee' heading (primary and high) without saying what it is or how often it is paid; ask the school. Primary learners pay the compulsory PMYF, R130 a month. Savings: 5% on school fees for paying 12 months in advance, 2.5% for 6 months in advance, up to 10% for siblings from the third child. Stationery, uniforms, trips and photos are extra. Grade R aftercare is included.
