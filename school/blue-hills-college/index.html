About these fees

Independent school with boarding, Grade R to 12. 2026 fees for South African (national) students: Grade R R50,930 a year or R4,630 a month for 11 months (January to November); Grade 1-7 R68,145 a year or R6,195 a month for 11 months. Registration R4,000 (Grade R-7) or R6,000 (Grade 8-12), non-refundable. Grade 8-12 day scholars pay R5,590 to R6,100 a month and boarders R11,585 to R12,260 a month (11 payments, a larger first payment in January), plus a R7,000 development levy, R4,000 modules and stationery and a R2,500 sports levy a year. International students pay more. 5% off if paid in full by 31 January 2026; 5% sibling discount on tuition.
