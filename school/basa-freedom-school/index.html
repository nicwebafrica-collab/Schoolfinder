About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. The 2026 page says there is no registration fee. Grade R: R880 a month, R10,560 a year (Grade 1 the same). Fees rise to R1,500 a month in Grade 4-7 and R2,652 a month in Grade 11-12. Grades R to 9 pay over 12 months; Grades 10 to 12 pay over 10 months. Fees are paid in advance, due by the 1st of each month. Brothers and sisters: a 3% sibling discount is printed from the second child, for example Grade R R850 a month, R10,200 a year. The sheet printed early-payment discounts for the full annual fee: 15% before 31 December 2025, 10% before 31 January 2026, or one month off before 28 February 2026; those dates have passed, so ask the school if they apply for 2027.
