About these fees

Independent school; the fee sheet does not say which grades it covers (the DBE lists it as a special needs school). Annual fees R201,840 per year, one fee for all grades. Pay it as R67,280 a term (due 1 January, 1 May and 1 September 2026) or R16,820 a month for 12 months (January to December, in advance on the 1st). Paid in full before 31 January 2026, 5% (R10,092) comes off, giving R191,748. A non-refundable development levy of R42,000 is payable on enrolment (shown here as registration). Every learner also pays R250 annual insurance and a R1,050 tours and magazine levy, both in January. Grade 8 and 9 learners pay a club/skills levy of R5,000 a term. A book pack will be invoiced in January; the sheet says the price is not yet finalised.
