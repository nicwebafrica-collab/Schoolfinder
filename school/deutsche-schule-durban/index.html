About these fees

German/English primary school. Grade 1 shown (Gr 1-3). Pay yearly, termly or 11 debit orders. Discount if paid once-off by 31 Jan 2026 (Gr 1-7 R87,160). Sibling discounts. Fees INCLUDE tuition, accident insurance, all sport, cultural tours and outings, and workbooks. The 'deposit' is a non-refundable R4,000 enrolment fee. The website also shows a termly table that doesn't match the annual table; confirm with the school. The school's page also lists an annual fee of R2,200 to R12,600 whose purpose isn't stated; confirm with the school.
