About these fees

Fee-paying public high school. Grade 8 and new learners: R77,370 for 2026 in all, made up of a non-refundable R4,000 advance payment (paid in 2025) plus R73,370 (R6,670 x 11, January to November). Grades 9-12: R73,370 (R6,670 x 11). Upfront discounts (school fees only, cash or EFT): 7.5% by 31 Dec 2025 down to 4% by 28 Feb 2026; card payments and scholarship holders get no discount. Textbooks, stationery, uniforms, sport equipment, tours, functions and cultural evenings are extra and not priced.
