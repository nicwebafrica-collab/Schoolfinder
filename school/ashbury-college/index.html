About these fees

Independent school, Gr RR to Gr 12. 2027 fees, monthly in advance: Gr RR-R R2,325 a month (R27,900 a year; all-inclusive, with lunch and aftercare to 17:30); Gr 1-7 R4,050 (R48,600); Gr 8-11 R4,525 (R54,300); Gr 12 R5,000 over 11 months (R55,000). Entrance fee R1,000 once-off for new learners (shown as registration). Re-registration R500 each year for current learners, before 31 October. A development levy of R2,550 is listed for Prep and High School, due before 31 January 2027; it is listed as once-off (ask the school if it recurs). Aftercare for Gr 1-7 R1,000 a month over 11 months (R11,000). Discounts: R50 per child per month for debit order; 5% for siblings (oldest pays full); 10% if the year is paid in full before 31 January 2027. Fees are 'for 2027 and subject to change'.
