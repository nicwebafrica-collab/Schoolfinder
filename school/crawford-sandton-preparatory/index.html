About these fees

Independent preparatory school, Grade 1 to 7. 2027 tuition is printed three ways: an annual (discounted) price if paid in full by 31 January 2027, or 12 monthly payments, or 10 monthly payments. The annual price is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so only the 12-month monthly amount is shown. Application fee R880 and non-refundable enrolment fee R13,200 are for new learners, paid through the school's online portal. Aftercare is optional: full day (to 17:30) R26,844 for the year, half day (to 15:00) R21,252 for the year, both discounted annual prices. An extra '30 min' option R8,868 for the year is not named on the sheet; ask the school.
