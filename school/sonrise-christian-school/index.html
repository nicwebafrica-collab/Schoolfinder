About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to Grade 12. 2027 fees are monthly, payable over 12 months. Headline is Gr RR: tuition R1,990 plus workbooks R60, total R2,050 a month. Other totals a month: Gr R R3,650; Gr 1 R5,375; Gr 2-3 R5,940; Gr 4-7 R6,070; Gr 8-9 R6,125; Gr 10-12 R6,165. R100 off tuition if paid by the 1st of the month. The page prints an enrolment fee of R500 in the fees section and R1,500 in the enrolment steps, plus a R500 application fee; the school's page gives both R500 and R1,500; ask the school. Special Projects Fund R80 a month per family. Sibling discount R200. Fees include Art, Music, Kidi-Sportz, prize-giving, R210 per term for outings and an annual camp for Gr 4-12; sport tournaments and buses are extra. Workbook costs may vary slightly.
