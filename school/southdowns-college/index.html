About these fees

Independent college (Crawford International), Grades 8 to 12; the sheet covers the college only. The 2027 sheet prints a discounted annual price if paid in full by 31 January 2027: Gr 8-9 R129,972; Gr 10-12 R142,632. Monthly options: for 10 months, Gr 8-9 R13,680 and Gr 10-12 R15,010; for 12 months, Gr 8-9 R11,631 and Gr 10-11 R12,776 (no 12-month option for Gr 12). The monthly fee we show is the 12-payment option (Gr 12 has only the 10-payment option, R15,010 a month for 10 months); the sheet prints no undiscounted yearly fee, so we show no yearly total. New learners pay a R500 application fee (registration) and a R5,000 enrolment fee (deposit), both once-off and non-refundable. Grade 12 also pays IEB exam fees of R10,394, billed separately by the end of March 2027. Sibling discounts: 5%, 10% and 15% for the second, third and fourth child.
