About these fees

Independent primary school, Grade R to Grade 7. 2026 fees for Grade R: R2,400 a month or R28,800 a year; Grade 1-3 R2,600 a month (R31,200 a year); Grade 4-6 R2,800 (R33,600); Grade 7 R2,900 (R34,800). Fees are paid in advance on or before the 1st of each month; anything received after the 1st carries a R200 penalty. Registration fee is R1,300. A stationery fee is listed at R2,600 for Grade R-3 and R3,000 for Grade 4-7, but the sheet does not say how often it is paid. The sheet also prints a December fee (R1,000 Grade R, R1,200 Grade 1-3, R1,800 Grade 4-6, R1,900 Grade 7) and, in the acceptance block, a December fee of R4,000; these do not agree, so ask the school what is payable on acceptance and in December.
