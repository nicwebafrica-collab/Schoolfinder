About these fees

Independent college, Gr 8-12. Each grade has a discounted annual price (Gr 8 R173,016) that is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so the monthly figure shown is the normal 12-payment option (Gr 8 R15,480 a month). A 10-payment option is also printed (Gr 8 R18,210). Gr 12 has only a 10-payment option, R22,010 a month (the Gr 12 monthly shown is this 10-payment amount); its discounted annual price is R209,088. The annual discount is lost if not paid in full by 31 January 2027, and the account then moves to the 12-month option. New learners pay a R880 application fee and a R5,500 non-refundable enrolment fee, both through the school's online portal. No aftercare at this phase.
