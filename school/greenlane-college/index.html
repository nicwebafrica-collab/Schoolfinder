About these fees

Independent school, Grade 1 to 12 (no Grade R in the fee tables). Entry grades 1-3: R850 monthly tuition and an annual total of R8,500. By grade, monthly tuition rises from R850 to R2,100 in Grade 12 (annual total R21,000). The page does not say how many months the annual total covers. Payment is monthly (debit order available) or quarterly, but no quarterly amounts are printed. A once-off non-refundable registration fee is paid on admission: R600 for Grade 1-7, R700 Grade 8, R900 Grade 9, R1,000 Grade 10-11, R1,500 Grade 12. The page gives both the 3rd and the 7th as the monthly due date; ask the school. Fees are non-refundable once paid. The page also says 2027 fees will be communicated by September 2026.
