About these fees

Boys' public school. Day scholar fees shown, paid over 10 months by debit order. 5% off (R75,525) if paid in full by 31 Jan. Weekly boarding R84,600/yr, termly boarding R98,900/yr, ad-hoc weekend boarding R800. Grade 8 applications go through the GDE Admissions website. New day scholars also pay a R6,500 school deposit. Registration shown is the R6,500 development levy, paid once by every new learner.
