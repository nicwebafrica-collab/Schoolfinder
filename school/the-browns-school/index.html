About these fees

Public school for children with special educational needs. 2026 fee per first child: R25,200 (Gr 1-7, Aut 2-8, SN 1-6) or R27,300 (Pre-Primary and Aut 1). Second child R23,940 / R25,935, third child R22,680 / R24,570. Every family pays at least R2,000 per learner, even with a fee remission (it is part of the annual fee). 5% off if paid in full by 28 Feb 2026 or within 30 days of admission. Monthly plans must be settled by 31 Oct 2026.
