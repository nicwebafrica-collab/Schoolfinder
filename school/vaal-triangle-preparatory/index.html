About these fees

Independent primary school, Gr RR to Gr 7. 2027 fees paid in 12 monthly instalments: Gr RR-R (ECD) R2,450 a month, R29,400 a year, all-inclusive (breakfast, light lunch, aftercare, workbooks, stationery); Gr 1-7 R2,525 a month, R30,300 a year. Once-off registration for new learners: ECD R1,500 (shown as registration), Gr 1-7 R2,000. Gr 1-7 aftercare R1,500 a month over 11 months (R16,500). Discounts: R50 per child per month by debit order; 5% for siblings (oldest pays full); 10% if the year is paid in full before 31 January 2027. Fees are subject to annual review. The page lists no deposit or application fee.
