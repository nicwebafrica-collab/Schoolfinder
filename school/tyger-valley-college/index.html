About these fees

Independent school, Pre-Primary (Gr 0000-0), Preparatory (Gr 1-7) and High School (Gr 8-12). 2027 tuition, 12 monthly payments Jan-Dec: Gr 0000 R4,480; Gr 000 R5,750; Gr 00 R6,130; Gr 0 R6,960; Gr 1-3 R9,390; Gr 4 R10,360; Gr 5-6 R10,460; Gr 7 R11,220; Gr 8-9 R11,370; Gr 10-11 R12,500. Gr 12 pays 10 monthly payments of R14,720. The annual prices on the sheet are lower than 12 monthly payments added up and apply only if paid by 31 January 2027, so no yearly figure is shown. Non-refundable enrolment fee R6,000 (shown as registration) and application fee R600. Textbooks and outings are included. Gr 12 IEB exam fees are billed separately (amount not stated). Optional aftercare is billed per term (pre-primary R910 a month, Gr 1-7 R1,590). Morning bus R1,030 a month (Gr 1-11, route to be announced). Sibling discount from the 2nd child: 5%, 10%, then 15%.
