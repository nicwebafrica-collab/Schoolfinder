About these fees

Independent preparatory school, Grade 1 to 7. 2027 tuition is printed three ways: an annual (discounted) price if paid in full by 31 January 2027, or 12 monthly payments, or 10 monthly payments. The annual price is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so only the 12-month monthly amount is shown. Application fee R880 and non-refundable enrolment fee R13,200 are for new learners, paid through the school's online portal. Rows headed Full Day (R26,844 for the year) and Half Day (R21,252 for the year) are discounted annual prices, shown as optional aftercare. An unnamed 'Option' for Grades 4-7 is also printed; ask the school what it is.
