About these fees

Independent school; 2027 fees. Pay annually (by 31 December 2026), termly in 3 payments, or monthly in 11 debit orders (January to November). The annual price is lower than the termly and monthly totals, so it is a pay-in-full price; the monthly amounts shown are for 11 payments. Gr 13 is printed with its own lower fee. Non-refundable enrolment fee on acceptance: R4,500 for senior school (Gr 8 and up). A top-up enrolment fee applies when moving to a new phase; no amount printed. Aftercare (unless extended day), holiday care, outside extramurals and Cambridge exam costs are extra, no amounts printed. One term's written notice required.
