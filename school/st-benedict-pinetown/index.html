About these fees

Catholic co-ed school, IEB matric. Grade 1 shown. 2027 fees: pay yearly, termly in advance (25% by 15 January, then on the first day of each later term), or 11 debit orders (1 Feb to 1 Dec). 5% off if paid in full by 31 Jan 2027 (e.g. Gr 1 R53,200); paying in full by 30 Nov 2026 is charged at the current rate. Sibling discount (amount not stated). Fees include a development levy, some special activities and accident insurance. Gr 12 tuition excludes the R10,500 IEB fee. Gr 3 remedial (REM) is R85,800 a year against R80,453. R350 non-refundable application fee. The 'deposit' is a non-refundable R5,000 acceptance fee. One term's notice to leave, otherwise a term's fees are payable.
