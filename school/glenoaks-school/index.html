About these fees

Independent school for learners with special needs. Fees are set by class, not grade: Remedial (Grade 1-7) R225,600 a year (R18,800 a month); ACORN R230,400 (R19,200); SVP R228,000 (R19,000); GOAL R228,000 (R19,000); Academy PVP Year 2 R225,600 (R18,800); Academy Year 3-5 R228,000 (R19,000). The yearly figure shown is the Remedial class. Once-off registration R17,500 per family on enrolment. Everyone also pays a capital development levy and a sport levy. Pay by 31 January, termly (3 payments), or over 11 or 12 months; the monthly amounts differ by option, so no single monthly is shown (see the sheet). Siblings get 10% off school fees. Compulsory stationery is listed with no amount. Aftercare is optional, per day: homework club without lunch R50, with lunch R70, full aftercare R90, ad-hoc emergency attendance R120; late pick-up R50 per 30 minutes.
