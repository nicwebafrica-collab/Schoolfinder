About these fees

Independent school (Royal Schools group). 2027 fees by grade; the headline is the youngest class (Gr R-7). Annual fee R27,840, or 12 monthly payments of R2,320. Paying the whole year by 31 January saves 5%; quarterly debit orders save 3.5%. Grade 12 is paid over 10 months (R3,750 a month). Fees include textbooks. Registration fee R1,500 for new learners only, not refunded if the application is declined or withdrawn. Sibling discount R210 for the 2nd, 3rd and 4th child. Aftercare is outsourced: ask the school office. Late payment and unpaid debit order fees R130.
