About these fees

Independent day and boarding school (Pre-school to Gr 12). Grade 1 shown. Fees can be paid over 10 months (January to October). Fees include all curriculum subjects, class music, PE, swimming, computer education and sport. A yearly General Purpose Levy is charged in January on top (R4,650 in Gr 1-3), covering accident insurance, basic textbooks, basic stationery, photos, the magazine, school trips and camps and most compulsory outings. 'Registration' is the non-refundable R400 application fee (R100 extra if late); 'deposit' is the non-refundable R3,250 enrolment fee (R250 extra if late). Boarding R91,000 a year. One full term's notice to leave.
