About these fees

Independent day and boarding school (Pre-school to Gr 12). Youngest group (Gr RRR-R) shown. Fees can be paid over 10 months (January to October). A yearly General Purpose Levy is charged in January on top (e.g. R4,650 in Gr 1-3), covering accident insurance, basic textbooks, trips, camps and most compulsory outings. 'Registration' is the non-refundable R400 application fee (R100 extra if late); 'deposit' is the non-refundable R3,250 enrolment fee (R250 extra if late). Current Gr 7 learners pay a refundable R2,000 enrolment fee to secure Gr 8, refunded in January and forfeited if they do not return. Boarding R96,000 a year (R24,000 a term), plus a one-term boarding enrolment fee paid in advance. Gr 12 pays the IEB exam fee of R10,394. Parents are asked to have medical aid. Overdue accounts pay 10% interest a year.
