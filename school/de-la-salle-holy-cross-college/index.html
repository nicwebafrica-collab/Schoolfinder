About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to Grade 12. Fees differ by grade; the lowest grade (RR) is R84,000 a year, up to R140,500 for Grade 12. Pay per year, per term (due on the first day of term: January, May and September) or per month for 10 months (January to October); for Grade RR that is R28,000 a term or R8,400 a month. A capital levy of R3,500 per family is charged each January, due by March. Extra subject costs apply to some learners (AP Maths, Visual Arts, IT and CAT, Geography). Some extra-subject amounts are not labelled by subject on the sheet (Gr 7 and Gr 8-9: R250 and R300; Gr 10: R1,500 and R930); ask the school. Junior School phone: 011 782 5217. Discounts: paying the full annual fee by EFT earns 5% if received by 15 December 2025, or 4% by 6 February 2026; the third child's annual fee is reduced by 25%, and a fourth child pays no annual fee, when all are enrolled together. The sheet lists no registration fee and no book pack price.
