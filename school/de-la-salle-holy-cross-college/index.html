About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to 12. Fees differ by grade, from R84,000 a year (Gr RR) to R140,500 (Gr 12). Pay per year, per term (due on the first day of term: January, May and September) or per month for 10 months (January to October); for Gr RR that is R28,000 a term or R8,400 a month. Capital levy R3,500 per family each January, due by March. Extra subject costs for some learners (AP Maths, Visual Arts, IT and CAT, Geography); some amounts aren't labelled by subject (Gr 7 and Gr 8-9: R250 and R300; Gr 10: R1,500 and R930), so ask the school. Junior School phone: 011 782 5217. Discounts: full annual fee by EFT earns 5% if received by 15 December 2025, or 4% by 6 February 2026; when all are enrolled together, the third child's annual fee is reduced by 25% and a fourth child pays no annual fee. The sheet lists no registration fee and no book pack price.
