About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to 12 plus a post-matric A Level year. 2027 tuition: Grade RR R4,960 a month or R59,520 a year, rising by grade to R9,400 a month / R112,800 a year for A Level. Twelve monthly instalments, 30 December to 30 November, paid in advance on the 30th. 'Registration' is the R3,500 once-off registration fee. Curriculum fee (PACEs) for Prep: Gr RR R2,200, Gr R-7 R8,500; called annual but also said to be paid per term, without the split. Once-off: application R500, diagnostic testing (Prep) R650. Discounts: annual payment by 1 December 10%, by 31 December 7%, twice-yearly 4%; siblings 5% (2nd), 10% (3rd). Aftercare R1,300 full-time, R850 half-day, how often not stated; ask the school. Cambridge exams: R1,050 (Gr 8) to R3,900 (A Level) per subject, about R3,150 to R18,900 a year (school's estimate). Cambridge textbooks extra (no amount).
