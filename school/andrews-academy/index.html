About these fees

Independent school, Grade RR to Grade 12 plus a post-matric A Level year. 2027 tuition for Grade RR: R4,960 a month, or R59,520 for the year; it rises by grade up to R9,400 a month / R112,800 a year for A Level. Twelve monthly instalments from 30 December to 30 November, paid in advance on the 30th. 'Registration' is the R3,500 once-off registration fee. Curriculum fee (PACEs) for Prep: Grade RR R2,200, Grades R to 7 R8,500; the page calls it annual but also says it is paid per term, without the split. Once-off: application R500, diagnostic testing (Prep) R650. Discounts: annual payment by 1 December 10%, by 31 December 7%, twice-yearly 4%; siblings 5% (2nd) and 10% (3rd). The page lists aftercare (R1,300 full-time, R850 half-day) without saying how often; ask the school. Cambridge exams: R1,050 (Gr 8) to R3,900 (A Level) per subject, about R3,150 to R18,900 a year (school's estimate). Cambridge textbooks are extra (no amount).
