About these fees

Independent Waldorf school, Baby Care to Class 12 (Gr R shown as the entry grade; Gr R tuition R105,732 a year, R9,612 a month for 11 months, January to November). Levies are extra, per child each year: infrastructure R1,900 and pupil insurance R205, plus a class fund (R216 for Gr R); Gr 1 and up add a sports levy of R1,450 and a larger class fund. First month's fee including levies for Gr R is R11,933. Discount: 5% if the annual total is paid by 30 November 2026 (Gr R R102,766); the sheet gives both 3.5% and 5% for 31 January 2027; ask the school. Registration fee R700 per child; enrolment fee R10,000 for entering Class 1 to 12 (the page does not say about Gr R). Aftercare is optional, billed monthly for 11 months; amounts are on the school's page.
