About these fees

Independent school (College, Grades 8 to 12 and a Sixth Form year). 2027 tuition R248,118 a year, or R85,267 a term, or R24,185 a month over 11 months. Annual fees are due by 31 January 2027; termly fees on the first day of each term; monthly fees only by debit order over 11 months (February to December), so paying monthly or termly costs more than the annual fee. On acceptance a refundable deposit of one term's tuition is paid, topped up on promotion. Levies for all grades are extra (development R12,648, security R1,690, PA projects R1,138 a year). Rowers pay R12,124 a year; Wilderness School R10,650 once in Grade 9 (LIV). Boarding is extra: weekly R142,781 or termly R175,010 a year, with a deposit of one term's boarding. Bus service R5,032 a term (optional).
