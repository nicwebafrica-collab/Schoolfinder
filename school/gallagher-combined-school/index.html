About these fees

Independent combined school, Grade R to 12. The page is titled 'Fees, costs and discounts 2027' (the web address says 2024). Grades R-3: R27,500 full year, R15,000 half year (2 payments), R7,500 a quarter (4 payments) or R2,500 a month (11 payments). Gr 4-6: R29,260 / R15,960 / R7,980 / R2,660. Gr 7-9: R33,000 / R18,000 / R9,000 / R3,000. Gr 10: R38,500 / R21,000 / R10,500 / R3,500. Gr 11: R39,380 / R21,480 / R10,740 / R3,580. Gr 12: R44,000 / R24,000 / R12,000 / R4,000. Once-off yearly, half-yearly and quarterly packages are paid on the last day of the previous month; otherwise payment is month by month. Registration for new learners is R800, non-refundable unless the school declines the registration. After-school care is R860 a month or part month. The page has a discounts heading but prints no discount amounts. Bus transport has an extra fee but no amount is given. Class sizes: maximum 25 learners in Gr R-9, 15 in Gr 10-12.
