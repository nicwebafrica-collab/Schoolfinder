About these fees

Public school, Gr R to Gr 7. One 2026 school fee for all grades, per child: R43,780 a year, or R3,980 a month by debit order over 11 months (January to November). The sheet also offered a discount for paying in full by 31 December 2025 (R550) or by 31 January 2026 (R500); both dates have passed and the discounted amounts are left out. Optional aftercare, per child, paid over 10 months (February to November): 12:45-14:30 R883 a month, 14:30-17:30 R1,235 a month, 12:45-17:30 R1,391 a month. Optional music lessons are also charged per child (for example group half hour R502 a month, individual one hour R1,416 a month). No registration or application fee is printed.
