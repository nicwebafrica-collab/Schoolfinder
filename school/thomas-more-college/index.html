About these fees

2027 fees, Grade 1 shown. Paid yearly, termly, or monthly over 10 months (Jan-Oct); pre-primary can also pay over 12 months (e.g. Gr 0000 R4,200/month). 5% off if paid by 4 Dec 2026, 4% by 15 Jan 2027. Fees include textbooks, grade tours and trips, insurance and sport coaching. NOT included: photocopying, workbooks, notebooks, exams and tests, benchmarking, AP Maths/English and AEU support. The 'deposit' is a non-refundable R18,000 facilities levy for new pupils (R7,000 upfront to secure the place, R11,000 on terms).
