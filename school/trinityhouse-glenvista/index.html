About these fees

Independent combined school (pre-primary, preparatory, high school); 2027 fees. Pay annually before 31 January 2027, or monthly in 12 payments due on the 1st. The annual price is lower than 12 monthly payments, so it is a pay-in-full price; monthly amounts shown are the 12-payment ones. Gr 12 has only 10 monthly payments (January to October) of R14,660. Bridging Classes Gr 1-3: R9,640 a month (R106,068 annual). Non-refundable application fee R600 and enrolment fee R6,000. Outing levy (Gr 1-7) R730 non-refundable, listed without saying how often; ask the school. Bus Gr 12 for 10 months: Route 1 R1,160, Route 2 R830. Late collection after 18H00: R250 per 15 minutes or part. Gr 12 has only the 10-payment option, R14,660 a month for 10 months.
