About these fees

Independent school, Grades 1-12. 2026 full year: Gr 1-6 R8,400; Gr 7-9 R10,800; Gr 10-12 R14,400. Continuing learners may pay monthly for 12 months: R700, R900 and R1,200. Fees are charged per term (R2,100, R2,700 and R3,600). New learners pay three months of fees on registration. Once-off new registration: Gr 1-6 R500, Gr 7-9 R700, Gr 10-12 R1,000; yearly re-registration R200 for every grade. International students pay a non-refundable application fee of R6,000, not part of the fees. Fees exclude incidentals, sports and tours, to be advised. Uniform prices (for example Gr 1-6 boys' full set R540, winter jacket R250, beanie R50) are listed without saying how often; ask the school.
