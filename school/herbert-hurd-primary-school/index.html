About these fees

State school (Quintile 5, fee-charging). 2027 school fee resolution accepted by parents at the AGM on 17 November 2026: one annual fee for all grades, R30 360.00, payable on the first day of Term 1 2027, or R2 760 a month, paid before the 7th of each month, 11 payments from January to November 2027. Full-payment discounts, only if paid in full by the date: 10% by 31 December 2026, 7% by 31 January 2027, 5% by 28 February 2027. Aftercare R1 000 a month (14:00 to 17:30). Music fees are extra: individual lesson R180 a month, brass and wind instrument hire R180 a month, reeds R65 a month (each over 11 months), remedial lesson R200 a term. Bus fares may be charged if used. A stationery fee is charged but no amount is given. Fee exemption can be applied for.
