About these fees

Independent school. 2027 fees from 1 January 2027, by grade; the headline is the youngest class (Gr 00-0). Standard annual fee R63,000, or 10 monthly payments of R6,300. Paying the whole discounted annual fee (R59,850) by 31 January 2027 saves money; a 12 monthly option of R5,360 is also printed (higher in total). Grade 12 has no 12 monthly option. Application fee R500 and enrolment fee R4,000 are once-off and non-refundable. Grade 12 IEB exam fees R10,394 are billed separately. Stationery, books and tours are paid by parents; amounts not printed. Aftercare is run privately by another company; no prices printed.
