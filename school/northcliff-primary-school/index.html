About these fees

Public primary school. The page shows 2026 annual fees: Gr R R38,600 (the Grade R Centre is described as private, with compulsory fees); Gr 1 to Gr 7 R27,000. The page does not say how many instalments the year is split into. On acceptance a new learner pays one month's fees (Gr R R3,860; Gr 1 to 7 R2,700) plus a building donation of R2,000; Gr 1 also pays a Grade 1 levy (intern salary) of R2,000, and Gr 1 to 7 a book deposit of R500. The page prints totals on acceptance of R5,860 (Gr R), R7,200 (Gr 1) and R5,200 (Gr 2 to 7). The building donation is counted as a once-off start-up cost; the Grade 1 levy and book deposit are listed without saying how often, so ask the school. No 2027 fees are on the page.
