About these fees

Independent IEB high school, Grade 8 to 12. Tuition for 2027 is printed per year and per month (payable over 12 months), and differs by grade: Gr 8-9 R75,600 (R6,300 a month), Gr 10 R80,400 (R6,700), Gr 11-12 R87,600 (R7,300). Monthly fees are due on or before the 7th. Once-off Application Fee R800 (Gr 8 to 12). Once-off Registration Fee R4,800, payable on registration and non-refundable. An Annual Development Fund of R1,950 per learner is payable in November 2026. Family discounts: 5% for the second child, 10% for the third and more. Upfront yearly payment discounts on tuition: paid before 31 October 2026, you pay 2026 rates; before 30 November 2026, 5% off 2027 fees; before 31 December 2026, 2.5% off.
