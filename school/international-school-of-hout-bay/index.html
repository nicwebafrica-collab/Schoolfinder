About these fees

Independent IB school, fees 'with effect 1st January 2026', Nursery to DP 2. Headline is Nursery (meals included); lunch is included from Early Years 1 to DP 2. Yearly is the plain annual fee; the sheet also gives a termly fee (4 terms) for each grade. The monthly amount is for 10 debit orders (January to October) and is higher than the annual fee spread over 10 months because the sheet adds a 2.5% surcharge to that option, so no instalment count is shown. The 3.5% discount for paying the year by 31 December 2025 has passed. Application fee R800 (once, non-refundable). Placement fee R12,500 once-off per student, due within 7 days of a letter of offer, Early Years 1 to DP 2. A R3,000 re-enrolment fee is invoiced in October and credited to January tuition if the child returns. Textbooks, camps and exam costs are not priced.
