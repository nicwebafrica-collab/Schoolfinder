About these fees

Public school. 2026 fees per pupil per year: Gr R R36,170 (R3,225 of this would already have been paid); Gr 1 R30,453; Gr 2-3 R28,673; Gr 4 R29,913; Gr 5-6 R29,733; Gr 7 R34,773. Fees are paid in monthly payments from January (Gr 5-6 January to November, others January to October with a slightly different last month), but the monthly lines were partly cut off in our copy, so monthly amounts and instalments are left out. The sheet offered a full-payment discount (R1,810 Gr R, R1,410 Gr 1-7) with a deadline printed as 28 February 2025 (a typo on the 2026 sheet); treat as passed. Stationery, voluntary sport camps or tours and Gr 7 costs (jacket, farewell) are not included.
