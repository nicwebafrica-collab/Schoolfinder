About these fees

Independent high school; the mid-year 2026 sheet prints Gr 8-12 only, so only high-school fees are shown and the headline is Gr 8-9. Monthly fee (12 payments, due on the 1st in advance), with the 12-payment totals the sheet prints: Gr 8-9 R8,600 (R103,200); Gr 10 R9,600 (R115,200); Gr 11 R10,400 (R124,800); Gr 12 R11,100 (R133,200). Paying per term or in full costs less; those options are on the school's sheet. Enrolment fee (non-refundable, due on acceptance) for Gr 8-9 is R5,000; Gr 10 and 11 R6,300. The Gr 12 enrolment fee is left out because the table (R6,850) and its conditions text (R6,300) disagree. The half-price enrolment offer ended 31 August 2026 and the 2025 payment deadlines have passed.
