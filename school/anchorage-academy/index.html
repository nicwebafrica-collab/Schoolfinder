About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 9. 2027 school fees: R4,200 a month for 12 months, payable before the 7th of each month (the school prints no yearly total). Registration R2,500, once-off and non-refundable, for new enrolments only. Every grade pays a compulsory book levy (R480) and essentials (R480) once a year, by 31 December 2026. Stationery and textbooks are bought through the school's order form; the sheet's totals with them are Grade R R2,610, Grade 1-3 R3,460, Grade 4 R4,020, Grade 5 R4,040, Grade 6 R4,130, Grade 7 R4,830, Grade 8 R4,975, Grade 9 R5,070. Aftercare R900 a month for 12 months, optional.
