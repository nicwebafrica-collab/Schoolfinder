About these fees

Independent school from Gr RRR to Gr 12. Yearly totals include tuition, a general-purpose levy (per pupil) and a R8,140 development levy that is charged per family, not per child, so a second child costs less than the table total. Gr RRR shown; Gr 8 is R174,240 a year (R15,840 x 11). Paid by debit order monthly over 11 months. 'Registration' is the non-refundable R800 application fee. 'Deposit' is the non-refundable acceptance fee of 10% of the annual tuition (R5,280 for Gr RRR, R15,180 for Gr 8; boarders also pay 10% of the boarding fee). Pupils from outside South Africa pay a R2,500 yearly surcharge and a deposit of two terms' fees. Discounts: 6.5% if paid in full before 30 Nov 2025, 3.5% before 31 Dec 2025; 10% off tuition for the second and later siblings in Gr 1-12. Uniform, transport, Parents' Association levies and extras like music are not priced.
