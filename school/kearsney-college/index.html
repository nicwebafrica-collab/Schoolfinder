About these fees

Boys' boarding and day school. 2027 day fee for Gr 8-9 shown: R268,180 a year, billed as R24,380 a month by compulsory debit order, January to November. Gr 10-11 R293,700 (R26,700 a month). Gr 12 R293,700 (R26,700 a month), or R29,370 a month if paid over ten months (compulsory from 2030). Boarding instead of the day fee: Gr 8-9 R401,390 a year; Gr 10-12 R426,910. Acceptance fee R48,100 per pupil, once, non-refundable (registration); once-off application fee R1,050. Non-SA pupils also pay a refundable deposit of three months' fees. Advance-payment discounts of 5.5%, 4.5% or 3.5% apply only if the whole year is paid by 30 Nov 2026, 31 Dec 2026 or 29 Jan 2027, so they are not in the headline. Brothers: 5% off each for two, 10% for three or more. The optional Parents' Society levy is left out (not confirmed on both reads).
