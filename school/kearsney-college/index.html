About these fees

Boys' boarding and day school. 2026 day fee for Gr 8-9 shown (R22,830 x 11, Jan-Nov debit order); Gr 10-12 R275,000 (Gr 12 may pay R27,500 x 10). Boarding instead: Gr 8-9 R375,870, Gr 10-12 R399,740. 'Registration' is the non-refundable R1,050 application fee; 'deposit' is the non-refundable R45,000 acceptance fee. Non-SA pupils also pay a refundable deposit of three months' fees. Upfront discounts 5.5% / 4.5% / 3.5% depending on payment date; brothers 5% each (two) or 10% each (three or more).
