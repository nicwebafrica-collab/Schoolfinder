About these fees

Independent senior school (Gr 8-12) on the Nova Pioneer Ruimsig campus; 2027 fees, published 20 May 2026. Pay annually (by 31 December 2026), termly in 3 payments, or monthly in 11 debit orders. The annual price is lower than the termly and monthly totals, so it is a pay-in-full price; the monthly amounts shown are for 11 payments (Gr 8 R9,630; annual R89,650). Non-refundable enrolment fee on acceptance: R4,500 for senior school (Gr 8 and up); a top-up applies when moving from Gr 7 into Gr 8. International exam costs are extra, no amounts printed. Family discount from the 2nd child (10%, 20%, 25%, 30%). One full term's written notice required.
