About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. 2027 school fees are paid over 12 months, January to December. Grade R is R3,590 a month (R43,080 for the year); Grade 1 R3,910; Gr 2-3 R4,170; Gr 4-7 R4,360; Gr 8 R4,630; Gr 9 R4,990; Gr 10-12 R5,510 a month (see the grade list). 'Registration' is the non-refundable enrolment fee, R3,130 for Grade R to 12, payable on approval of the application. The primary compulsory PM YF levy is R170 a month. 5% off school fees if paid for any 12 months in advance, 2.5% off if paid for any 6 months in advance, and up to 10% for siblings from the third child. Debit orders are scheduled monthly in advance for 12 months. Stationery, textbooks and workbooks are for the parent's account, listed 'as per list', no amounts given.
