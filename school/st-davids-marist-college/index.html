About these fees

Independent school, Sandton campus, Gr 000 to Gr 12. 2027 monthly tuition over 11 payments (January to November): Gr 000 R8,680; Gr 00 R9,331; Gr 0 R12,188; Gr 1-2 R14,688; Gr 3 R15,916; Gr 4-7 R17,255; Gr 8-9 R19,646; Gr 10-11 R20,374; Gr 12 R22,412 over 10 months. The only annual price printed is a 4% discounted pay-in-full price, so no yearly figure is shown. A once-off non-refundable enrolment fee is paid on first enrolment (R20,000 for Gr 000; R15,000 to R30,000 by grade, see by-grade list). Application fee R500 for new applications. Annual development levy R13,542 for every learner. College boarding Gr 8-11: R146,634 annual or R13,886 monthly; Gr 12 over 10 months: R146,634 or R15,274; unclear if tuition is included. A Marist Institute Levy line has unclear amounts, so it is not listed. Aftercare month-by-month R2,243 over 9 months. Alexandra campus not covered.
