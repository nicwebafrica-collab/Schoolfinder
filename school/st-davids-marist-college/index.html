About these fees

Independent school, Sandton campus, Grade 000 to Grade 12, with boarding from Grade 8. The sheet prints a 4% discounted annual price (Grade 000 R85,821, due by 22 January 2026) and a monthly fee over 11 payments, January to November (Grade 000 R8,127); the full yearly fee is not printed, so no yearly figure is shown. Grade 12 pays over 10 months (R20,985). Monthly fee rises by grade; see the by-grade list. A non-refundable enrolment fee is paid once on first enrolment (R15,000 for Grade 000) and the application fee is R500. An annual development levy of R12,597 is charged to every learner in January. Boarding Grade 8-11 is R137,041 discounted annual or R12,977 monthly; the sheet doesn't say if it includes tuition. Third-party costs are billed in February and deducted over 3 months, no amounts printed. Aftercare R2,100 per month for 9 months. The Alexandra campus is not covered.
