About these fees

Independent primary school, Grade 1 to 7. 2027 fees: Gr 1-3 R92,050 a year, Gr 4-7 R93,500 a year. Or termly (Gr 1-3 R30,685, Gr 4-7 R31,180, due by the 7th of the month the term starts); 12 months (Gr 1-3 R7,680, Gr 4-7 R7,800 a month); or 11 months, January to November (Gr 1-3 R8,380, Gr 4-7 R8,500 a month), due by the 7th. 5% off for paying the year in full by 31 January; 5% sibling discount for the 2nd child. 'Registration' is the R400 non-refundable application and assessment fee. 'Deposit' is the R7,000 once-off, non-refundable enrolment fee per child, paid when the place is accepted; the page does not say if it comes off the fees. Compulsory yearly charges: consumables levy (Gr 1-2 R3,700 including stationery; Gr 3-7 R2,460, stationery excluded) and a R950 per family grounds levy in September. Optional: meals R1,700 a month, aftercare R1,900 a month, Flexi Care R200 a day, swim school.
