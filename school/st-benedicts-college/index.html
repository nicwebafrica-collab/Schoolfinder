About these fees

Independent boys' school, Grade 00 to Grade 12, with boarding from Grade 8. 2027 school fees by 13 January 2027, or a discounted upfront price by 13 November 2026, or termly, or monthly over 11 instalments (January to November). Entry level shown is Grade 00 Offering A (half day, no holiday care): R64,990 a year, R22,204 termly, R6,203 per month; Grade 1-3: R121,515 (upfront R115,440), termly R41,517, monthly R11,599. Higher grades are in the by-grade list. Monthly amounts add up to more than the yearly fee, so no monthly figure is shown. Enrolment is a once-off non-refundable R5,000 for Grade 00 (R15,000 from Grade 0, topped up) and the application fee is R750. Capital levy R6,800 a year for the first child (R5,270 second, R3,585 third); matric parents are exempt. Brother discounts: 5% for two children up to 20% for five.
