About these fees

Fee-paying public high school with boarding. Day tuition: Gr 8 R87,600 (includes a 'differential rate'), Gr 9-12 R82,100. Due by 31 January, or over 10 months (January to October or November) by debit order; the monthly figure is our calculation. The school's page is headed '2026 Fees', but a few lines still say 'Fees for 2025' with the same amounts. A R10,000 tuition deposit (50% refundable) is paid on acceptance; the page doesn't say if it comes off the fees. Estimated incidentals about R7,500 a year, not counting tours.
