About these fees

Independent Christian school, preschool to Grade 12 (Gr 10-12 are LCA). The 2026 schedule gives three options per grade: A over 12 months, B over 11 months, C the full year less 6.5% if paid by 31 December 2025. Preschool: A R2,355, B R2,570, C R26,425. Grade R: A R2,950, B R3,215, C R33,095. Gr 1: A R3,550, B R3,870, C R39,830. Other grades are in the by-grade rows; Gr 12 (LCA) prints only Option A, R6,395 a month. The monthly fee we show is Option A (12 payments); the yearly figure is a discounted one, so we show no yearly total. Tuition includes materials and levies; sport, transport, photos, stationery, tours and the nurse are extra. New learners pay a R160 application fee (registration) and a R1,300 enrolment fee (deposit). Aftercare is optional, R35 an hour or monthly options. New learners pay a PACE school-readiness diagnostic: Gr R R80, Gr 1-3 R315, Gr 4-12 R520.
