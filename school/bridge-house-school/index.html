About these fees

Independent school, Playschool to Grade 12 with boarding, 2027 fees. Playschool/Gr 000 tuition is R70,700 for the year (due 4 January 2027), or 4 termly payments of R18,330, or 11 monthly payments of R6,880 (debit order only). Monthly payments add up to more than paying the year upfront; other grades' monthly amounts are on the school's schedule. Gr 12 termly and monthly options run over three terms and 9 months. New learners pay a R500 application fee per family and a placement fee on acceptance: R4,000 (Playschool to Gr 0) or R8,000 (Gr 1-12). New Gr 1-12 learners also pay a non-refundable capital development levy: Gr 1-10 R21,500 once-off or R7,500 a year for three years; Gr 11 R15,000 or R7,500 a year; Gr 12 R7,500 once-off. Sibling rebate on tuition: 2.5% second child, 5% third child or more.
