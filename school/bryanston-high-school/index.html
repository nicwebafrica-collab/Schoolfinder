About these fees

Fee-paying public school. 2027 school fees: Grade 8 R62,500, Grades 9-12 R60,500 a year. Option 1: a first instalment of R5,000 by 30 November 2026, then ten equal payments from 31 January to October 2027 (Gr 8 R5,750, Gr 9-12 R5,550). Option 2: pay in full by 31 December 2026 and get 5% off the school fee (Gr 8 R3,125, Gr 9-12 R3,025 off). The fee table is a picture on the school's fees page.
