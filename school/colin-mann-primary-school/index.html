About these fees

Public primary school. 2026 annual fee: Gr R R31,130, Gr 1 R22,330, Gr 2-7 R19,195. Parents can pay monthly over 11 months (invoiced 31 January to 30 November 2026): Gr R R2,830, Gr 1 R2,030, Gr 2-7 R1,745. Or termly in the first week of each term: Gr R R7,782.50, Gr 1 R5,582.50, Gr 2-7 R4,798.75. Or one single payment by 31 March 2026 with 5% off: Gr R R29,574, Gr 1 R21,214, Gr 2-7 R18,236. There is no debit order; parents set up a stop order. The page lists no registration fee, aftercare, stationery or other levies.
