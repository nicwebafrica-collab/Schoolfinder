About these fees

Fee-paying public primary school, Grade R to Grade 7. Figures are for Grade R: R1,900 a month for 10 months, R19,000 per learner per year; no discount for Grade R. Grade 1-6: R1,590 a month for 11 months, R17,490 a year; R1,000 off if the full amount is paid before the end of January 2027, giving R16,490. Grade 7: R1,900 a month for 10 months, R19,000 a year; R1,000 off if paid before the end of January 2027, giving R18,000. First instalment due 7 January 2027, then the 1st of each month to the last month (October for Grade R and 7, November for Grade 1-6). The school prints no other charges.
