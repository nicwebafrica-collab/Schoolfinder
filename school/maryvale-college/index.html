About these fees

Independent school with a primary campus (Orchards) and a high school campus (Maryvale). 2027 fees, paid in 11 monthly payments from January to November, due by the 7th: Gr RRR to Gr 7 R2,545 a month; Gr 8 R3,000; Gr 9-10 R4,545; Gr 11-12 R5,000. The sheet also prints a 'tuition fees and levy included' figure for each grade (R27,995 for Gr RRR-Gr 7) without saying for what period; ask the school. Levies are included. Registration deposit R3,000, once-off and non-refundable, paid on application or re-enrolment. Optional aftercare is charged per day of attendance; the sheet shows R740 under the aftercare heading without explaining it; ask the school.
