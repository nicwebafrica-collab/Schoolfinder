About these fees

Independent school, Grade 000 to 12. 2027 fees are printed as a yearly fee and a monthly fee by phase. The yearly fee applies only if paid in full by 31 January 2027, so we show the monthly fee. Entry (Gr 000-0): yearly R90,550, monthly R8,200. Gr 1-3: R118,150 / R10,700. Gr 4-6: R123,650 / R11,200. Gr 7-9: R129,150 / R11,700. Gr 10-11: R136,350 / R12,350. Gr 12: R147,400 / R13,350 (includes IEB exam costs). Registration is a once-off R7,000, non-refundable. Sibling discount 10%. A yearly learner levy (R3,820 to R5,310 by grade) is charged at the start of each year. The school doesn't print how many monthly payments there are. One term's notice is needed to leave.
