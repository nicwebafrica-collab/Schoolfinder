About these fees

Independent primary school, Grade 1 to Grade 7. 2027 school fees for Grade 1: R7,860 a month, R94,320 for the year, payable over 12 months (January to December); Grade 7 is R9,230 a month. A non-refundable enrolment fee of R6,230 is payable when the application is approved. Savings: 5% on school fees for paying 12 months in advance, 2.5% for 6 months in advance, up to 10% for siblings from the third child. Textbooks, workbooks, stationery, excursions and sports trips are extra. Parents must replace lost books (no amount printed). Curro lists this campus under a combined DBE entry; the high school letter is a separate record.
