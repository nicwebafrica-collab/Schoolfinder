About these fees

Independent primary school, Gr R to Gr 7. 2026 tuition is R1,910 a month, the same for every grade, January to November. Book fees differ by grade and are charged at the start of the year: Gr R R990, Gr 1 R1,100, Gr 2 R1,220, Gr 3 R1,290, Gr 4 R1,300, Gr 5 R1,335, Gr 6 R1,360, Gr 7 R1,430. The school describes the book fee as once-off, payable at the start of the year; it is shown here as a yearly cost. No yearly total or discount is printed. The page says tuition covers Kinderkinetics (Gr R and 1), swimming, computer and extra science lessons.
