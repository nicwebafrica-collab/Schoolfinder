About these fees

Independent school; 2027 fees. Pay annually (by 31 December 2026), termly in 3 payments, or monthly in 11 debit orders (January to November). The annual price is lower than the termly and monthly totals, so it is a pay-in-full price; the monthly amounts shown are for 11 payments. Fees shown are for normal dismissal. Extended day with aftercare, meal and snacks for Gr 000 & 00: R6,090 a month (11 payments). Non-refundable enrolment fee on acceptance: R2,200 for pre-primary; R3,900 for primary (Gr R-7). A top-up enrolment fee applies when moving to a new phase; no amount printed. Aftercare (unless extended day), holiday care, outside extramurals and Cambridge exam costs are extra, no amounts printed. One term's written notice required.
