About these fees

Public school, fee-charging. 2026 school fees per annum: Gr 1-3 R27,500; Gr 4-7 R28,500. Early-payment discounts (10%, 7%, 5%, deadlines 31 December 2025 to 28 February 2026) have passed and the printed discounted amounts are the same on both sheets, so they are not used; discounts depend on paying by the dates printed. New learners pay a once-off development levy of R1,500 on registration and no e-levy; returning learners pay an e-levy of R300 (Gr 2-7). Book packs and textbook levies are listed per grade below (the sheet does not say 'yearly' outright; they are for 2026). Optional aftercare is R850 a month, supervision only (R1,450 with lunch), over 11 months, plus R100 a day casual.
