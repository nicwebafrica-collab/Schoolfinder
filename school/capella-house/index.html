About these fees

Independent primary school, Pre 1/2 to Grade 6. 2026 fees: Pre 1/2 R4,315 a month for 12 payments (5 January to 1 December), rising to R6,470 a month in Grade 6. The monthly amount includes the Development and Stationery Fee. Other options, also including that fee: 4 termly payments with a 3% discount (Pre 1/2 R12,617) or 1 annual payment with a 5% discount (Pre 1/2 R49,592); all grades are in the grade list. The sheet doesn't print the yearly fee before discount. A R7,000 Acceptance Fee (once-off, non-refundable) applies for children enrolling from 1 January 2024. Younger siblings get 7% off school fees and 50% off the Acceptance Fee. Bi-annual payment (5 January and 1 July) gets 3.5% off.
