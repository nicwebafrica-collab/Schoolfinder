About these fees

Independent school in Vanrhynsdorp; grades are not printed on the fee sheet. 2026 school fund for a single learner: R32,300 a year, or R3,230 a month in 10 equal instalments from February to November (the sheet words the annual amount as once-off). Two learners from the same household: R61,000 a year, or R6,100 a month over the same 10 months. A 5% discount applied to a full year paid on or before 5 February; that date has passed. All new enrolments pay a deposit of 10% of the yearly school fund on acceptance; it is not part of the fund and is not deducted from it. Payments are due by the 5th of each month. The school's separate pre-school (Kinderwerf) fees are not included here.
