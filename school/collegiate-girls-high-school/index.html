About these fees

Public girls' high school with a hostel. 2026 school fees R57,156 a year, or R5,196 a month for 11 months (January to November, debit order). Pay the year before 28 February 2026 and the fee is R51,440. New learners pay a R50 application administration fee and a R5,000 admission orientation registration fee. Hostel is extra: R87,000 a year for Grades 8-11 (R78,300 if paid before 28 February 2026) or R21,750 a term; Grade 12 R87,000 (R78,300) or R29,000 before each of the first 3 terms. Hostel leavers in Grades 8-11 who don't give the required notice pay a penalty equal to three months' fees (no rand amount printed). Music (instrument hire or practical music) is R480 a month for 10 months, R1,200 a term or R4,800 a year. Hospitality/Consumer Studies (Food) R550 a year.
