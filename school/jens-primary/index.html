About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 2. 2027 school fee R23,500: paid in full by 1 February 2027, or 4 x R5,875 a term, or 10 x R2,350 a month (February to November). 'Registration' is the R300 application fee for a new learner (R150 for a learner already at the school). 'Deposit' is the non-refundable R2,350 enrolment fee, paid on acceptance to secure the place, due 31 October 2026; the page doesn't say whether it comes off the 2027 fees. Every child also pays a R1,300 book fee, R600 sports, art and culture fee and R200 development fee (by 11 January 2027) and R100 photograph money (by 1 March 2027). Penalties: R100 a month on overdue fees, R100 for a cash payment, and late collection R300 per hour or part after 20 minutes' grace.
