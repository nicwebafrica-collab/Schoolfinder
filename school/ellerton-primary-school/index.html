About these fees

Public primary school. 2026 annual fee for new learners: Gr R R11,000, Gr 1-7 R10,000. A R2,000 registration payment on acceptance counts towards the annual fee; the balance is R9,000 (Gr R) or R8,000 (Gr 1-7), payable as R900 or R800 a month from January to October 2026. Existing learners pay R10,000: R2,000 before 15 December 2025 then R800 a month from January to October 2026, or no deposit and R1,000 a month from January to October 2026. Discounts, shown as percentages only: 5% off if the full account is paid before 28 February 2026, or a 5% sibling discount; only one of the two can be used. Monthly amounts are not shown as a figure because they cover only the balance after the R2,000.
