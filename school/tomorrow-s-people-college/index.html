About these fees

Independent school, pre-primary to Gr 12 with a special-needs section. 2026 fees are monthly only, charged over 11 months, paid in advance on the 2nd. Headline: Angels (Gr RRR) full day R5,015 (half day R4,746). Love/Joy (Gr RR) R5,697 (half day R5,404); Hope/Peace (Gr R) R5,799; Gr 1-6 R7,124; Gr 7 R7,245; Gr 8-9 R7,245; Gr 10-12 R7,523; special-needs groups R7,124 to R7,224. Non-refundable enrolment fee: pre-primary R2,000 (shown as registration), Gr 1-12 R7,500. School packs (Gr 1-9 R5,908 to R8,066) and extras (tours, camps, therapy, a tablet from Gr 4) are charged to the account. Gr 1-7 aftercare R1,465 a month. Special-needs groups pay extra per term for life skills (R300-R500) and, for some groups, transport (R350). Sibling discount 5% for the 2nd to 4th child. The early-payment discounts printed (to 15 January 2026) have passed. No 2027 schedule found.
