About these fees

Compulsory fee-paying public school, Grade R to Grade 3. 2026 annual school fees per learner: Grade R R33,300; Grade 1-3 R31,200, payable in advance or in 10 equal monthly payments from January to October 2026 (R3,120 a month for Grade 1-3). Grade R pays a R5,950 fee deposit, and Grade 1 and new learners a R3,950 deposit; these come off the year's fee, so they are not extra. Grade R then pays R2,735 a month for 10 months. Compulsory book fee R550 per learner. Discounts: R1,800 if paid in full by 31 December 2025, R1,400 by 31 January 2026, or R1,000 off in October after 10 successful debit orders.
