About these fees

Independent school, pre-primary (3-6 years) to Grade 7. Fees are billed monthly for 11 months, January to November. Printed totals per year: Pre-primary R57,178 (R5,198 a month), Grades 1-3 R79,035 (R7,185 a month), Grades 4-7 R86,845 (R7,895 a month). Paying the year in full by 5 February 2026 costs R54,320 (Pre-primary), R75,080 (Grades 1-3) or R81,860 (Grades 4-7). Enrolment fee R5,000 per child, non-refundable, all levels; the sheet doesn't say how often or whether it comes off the fees. Afterschool care per term: R3,000 (12:30-15:00), R3,000 (14:00-17:00), R3,800 (12:30-17:00); casual R95 per afternoon or part thereof. Individual music lessons (32 per year) R2,000 per term. Overdue accounts may carry surcharges.
