About these fees

Christian school, Pre Gr R to Gr 12. 2026 fees, entry grade Pre Gr R and Reception shown: R15,400 a year, paid as 11 monthly payments of R1,400 (January to November). Other grades: Gr 1-3 R1,750, Gr 4-6 R1,800, Gr 7-9 R1,900, Gr 10-12 R2,100 a month. No discount for paying the full year in advance. Registration R1,200 (it was R1,100 if paid by 5 December 2025), only if space is available; it covers textbooks, photocopies and stationery. Paying cash into the school account adds 1% bank charge; lost or damaged books are charged to the parent (no amounts printed, not in hidden costs). The school is cashless. The sheet has no 2027 fees yet.
