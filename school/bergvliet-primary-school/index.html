About these fees

Public primary school, Grade R to Grade 7, 2026 fees as printed on the school's page. Grade R is R35,105 for the year and Gr 1-7 R31,535. Options: pay termly (Gr R R8,776.25, Gr 1-7 R7,883.75, in the first two weeks of term), or for 11 months (Gr R R3,192, Gr 1-7 R2,867), or for 10 months (Gr R R3,510.50, Gr 1-7 R3,153.50). A 5% discount applies if paid by 28 February 2026 (Gr R R33,349.75, Gr 1-7 R29,958.25). The page does not say which fees are compulsory. Aftercare is R17,600 for the year, or R1,600 for 11 months, R1,760 for 10 months, or R4,400 a term. Music prices are in the extras. Camp: Gr 7 R2,210 (loaded on the Karri app); Gr 5 and 6 amounts not printed. These are 2026 fees; ask the school for 2027.
