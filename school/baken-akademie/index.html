About these fees

Independent school; the 2026 schedule covers Gr 000 to Gr 7 only (Gr 8-12 fees are on a separate college schedule, not read). Annual fee: Gr 000 R51,940, Gr R R65,720, Gr 1-3 R72,292, Gr 4-6 R78,864, Gr 7 R81,832. Two monthly options are printed, over 12 months or over 11 months (Gr 000: R4,328 or R4,722; Gr R: R5,477 or R5,975; Gr 1-3: R6,024 or R6,572; Gr 4-6: R6,572 or R7,169; Gr 7: R6,819 or R7,439). Once-off payment discounts are dated 2025 (before 15 October 2025 minus 9%, before 15 November minus 7%, before 15 December minus 5%; Gr 000 R46,820, R47,849, R48,878); ask the school if they still apply. Sibling discount: 8% for the 2nd child, 12% for the 3rd. A R3,350 capital fund per learner is payable by 1 December of the preceding year.
