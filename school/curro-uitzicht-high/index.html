About these fees

Independent high school, Grade 8 to Grade 12. 2027 school fees for Grade 8: R9,890 a month, R118,680 for the year, payable over 12 months (January to December); Grade 12 is R11,410 a month. A non-refundable enrolment fee of R6,230 is payable when the application is approved. Grade 12 learners who need an exam scribe or reader pay R460 per scribe or reader per exam sitting. Savings: 5% on school fees for paying 12 months in advance, 2.5% for 6 months in advance, up to 10% for siblings from the third child. Textbooks, workbooks, stationery, excursions and sports trips are extra. Parents must replace lost books (no amount printed).
