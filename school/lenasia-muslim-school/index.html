About these fees

Independent school, Grade 1 to Grade 12. Amounts depend on the number of children; the headline and the grade rows use the FIRST child. First child, Gr 1-6: R40,980 annually, or 4 termly instalments of R10,245, or 10 monthly instalments of R4,098 (January to October). First child, Gr 7-12: R44,220 annually, R11,055 termly or R4,422 monthly. Second child pays R37,907 (Gr 1-6) or R40,904 (Gr 7-12) annually, termly R9,477 or R10,226, monthly R3,791 or R4,090. Third, fourth or fifth child pays R36,882 (Gr 1-6) or R39,798 (Gr 7-12) annually, termly R9,221 or R9,950, monthly R3,688 or R3,980. Registration fee: non-refundable, amount not printed. A R100 admin fee is charged if a debit order is dishonoured.
