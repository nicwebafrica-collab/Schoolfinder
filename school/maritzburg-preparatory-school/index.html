About these fees

Independent school, Pre-School (Gr RRR/RR and Gr R) and Gr 1 to 4; Gr 5 is added in 2028, Gr 6 in 2029 and Gr 7 in 2030. Fees shown are for Pre-School Gr RRR/RR: R33,220 a year, or R3,020 a month (the sheet says 'Per Annum 11 months'; fees run 1 January to 1 November 2027). Fees differ by grade (see grade list). Every learner also pays medical insurance R240 and ISASA R240 (each once-off, in January) and Pop Up (outings, shows, events) R440 a year. A stationery fee is due by 1 November 2026 (Gr RR/RRR R1,450; Gr R R1,700; Gr 1 to 3 R2,050; Gr 4 R2,250); the sheet does not say how often, so ask the school. Optional aftercare: R750 a month for 10 months (February to November), or R75 per day casual. Early settlement discounts exist; contact the bursar before 1 December 2026. The sheet lists no registration or enrolment fee.
