About these fees

Public primary school in Goodwood, Cape Town. The 2026 fee letter (dated 10 November 2025) says school fees are R11,600 per learner per year, with a compulsory LTSM (books and essential goods) payment of R600 per learner due before 25 January 2026, and thereafter R1,100 a month per learner for 10 months (1 February to 1 November). The letter does not say whether the R600 is inside the R11,600, so the instalments count is left out, and the R600 is not listed as an extra. Early-payment discounts on the letter (before 31 December 2025, before 31 March 2026) have passed. No grades are listed.
