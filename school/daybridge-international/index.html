About these fees

Independent high school. The 2026 sheet (no 2027 sheet found) lists Foundation (Gr 8) at R81,600 for the year, or R6,800 a month over 12 instalments, and IGCSE/AS (Gr 9-12) at R86,400, or R7,200 a month. Pay annually in advance for 10% off (Foundation R73,440; IGCSE/AS R77,760), or per term in advance for 5% off (Foundation R19,380; IGCSE/AS R20,520). Sibling rates (less 10%): Foundation R66,100 a year, R17,445 a term, R6,120 a month; IGCSE/AS R69,990 a year, R18,470 a term, R6,480 a month. The sheet does not label the R81,600 and R86,400 figures; we read them as the full yearly fee because they match 12 times the monthly amount. A registration fee of R2,000 is charged each year. IGCSE/AS learners also pay an annual practical fee of R3,000.
