About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. 2027 tuition for the entry grade (Grade R): R810 a month (x12), or R2,430 a term, or R9,720 for the year. Fees by grade are printed in the same three ways: Gr 2-5 R900 a month; Gr 6-7 R1,000; Gr 8-9 R1,400; Gr 10-11 R1,500; Gr 12 R1,600. 'Registration' is the R1,200 administration fee for 2027, payable once off. The school says new students must pay the full monthly school fees on enrolment, including the R1,200 administration fee. No early-payment discounts are printed. Textbooks and stationery are for parents to buy (no amount printed); the school does not copy textbooks.
