About these fees

Independent combined school, Nursery to Year 12. Entry grade is Nursery: R59,235 a year, with effect from 1 January 2027 (Pre-Reception and Reception R65,864; see the by-grade list). Options: R57,162 if paid in full by 31 December 2026 (3.5% discount, Nursery), R14,809 a term for four terms, or R6,072 a month for 10 months, January to October 2027, which includes a 2.5% surcharge. Application fee R800 (non-refundable); placement fee R12,000 on acceptance (non-refundable); re-enrolment contract R3,000, deducted from the first invoice in January 2027. Foreign students pay a refundable R20,000 deposit. Aftercare, early care and exam fees (GBP, invoiced August 2027) cost extra. Sibling discounts: second child 5%, third 10%, fourth 30%. A 6.5% late penalty applies if tuition isn't paid by the 7th of the month.
