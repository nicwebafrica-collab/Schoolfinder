About these fees

2027 fees, Grade 8 shown; rises to R65,520/yr (R5,460/mo) in Gr 11-12. Paid over 12 months, January to December. 5% off for 12 months upfront, 2.5% for 6 months upfront, up to 10% sibling discount from the 3rd child. The R1,155 is the non-refundable high school enrolment fee. Textbooks are provided by the school (lost or damaged ones are charged); workbooks, exercise books and stationery are billed extra.
