About these fees

Fee-paying public school from Grade RR to Grade 12 (the fee page is in Afrikaans). 2026 fees for the first child: Gr RR-3 R23,917 a year or R2,004 a month by EFT; Gr 4-12 R27,789 a year or R2,329 a month by EFT. By debit order over 11 months (January to November): Gr RR-3 R1,924, Gr 4-12 R2,236 a month. The page doesn't say over how many months the EFT amount is paid. The page also lists discounts of 8% and 6% for paying early, but prints those dates as 2025, so check them with the school. Other children in the family may pay a different amount; ask the school.
