About these fees

Independent school, Gr 000 to Gr 12. 2027 tuition per year, Gr 000 R55,745; Gr 00 R61,475; Gr 0 R65,095; Gr 1-3 R87,585; Gr 4-6 R109,050; Gr 7 R119,220; Gr 8 R136,730; Gr 9 R140,990; Gr 10 R149,260; Gr 11 R149,800; Gr 12 R148,200. The sheet gives annual fees only; monthly debit orders run January to October (10 instalments), but the sheet prints no monthly amount, so none is shown. Levies are extra (see hidden costs); Gr 11 and 12 also pay IEB fees. Family discount: 5% for a second daughter, 7.5% for each further daughter. 5% off total annual fees if paid by 31 January 2027. Aftercare is charged per term (R500 to R5,000 depending on days and half or full day). Bus is separate: Westville R8,500 or Amanzimtoti R9,000 a year over 10 months. No registration or application fee appears on the sheet.
