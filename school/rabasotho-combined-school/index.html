About these fees

Independent combined school, Grade R to 12. 2027 fees are printed three ways for each phase: monthly, quarterly and annual. Foundation Phase (Gr R-3): R550 monthly, R1,650 quarterly, R6,600 annual. Intermediate (Gr 4-6): R600, R1,800, R7,200. Senior (Gr 7-9): R650, R1,950, R7,800. FET (Gr 10-12): R750, R2,250, R9,000. The number of instalments is not printed. Grade R registration is R750 with no assessment fee. Other grades: assessment fee R500 per learner and administration/registration fee R2,000 for new enrolments; the sheet does not say how often these are charged. Fees are non-refundable and non-transferable once a learner is registered.
