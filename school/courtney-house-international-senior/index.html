About these fees

Independent senior phase, Year 7 to Year 13. 2027 tuition for Year 7: R14,160 a month, R169,920 for the year, payable over 12 months; Year 12 is R19,110 a month. Year 13 (post-matric) is R4,700 per subject with no monthly or yearly total printed. A non-refundable enrolment fee is payable when the application is approved; its amount is not printed. The letter also lists R8,925 under a school fee heading (Years 7 to 13) without saying how often it is paid; ask the school. Cambridge exam fees are charged per subject (for example Year 8 Checkpoint R2,115) and the layout for IGCSE, AS and A Levels is unclear; ask the school. Transport: one-way R2,625 a month, return R3,490 a month. Savings: 5% for 12 months in advance, 2.5% for 6 months, up to 5% for siblings.
