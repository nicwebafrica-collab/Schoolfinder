About these fees

Independent school, Gr R to Gr 8. 2026 fees per learner per month: Gr R R450; Gr 1 to 8 R500. The page does not print a yearly total or say for how many months fees are charged. Registration is R750, once-off per learner when a child first enrols, the same for every grade. Pay by EFT or at the school office; ask the office for banking details. Parents who struggle to pay can ask the Principal early for a written payment arrangement. The school says its 2027 fees are not yet published.
