About these fees

Independent school, KG 1 to Grade 12 (IEB, with an IB option in Gr 11-12). 2027 fees by grade; the headline is the youngest class (KG 1-2): R108,480 a year if paid by 31 January 2027, or R107,350 if paid by 31 December 2026. Paying termly (3 x R37,667) or monthly (10 x R12,430, Jan-Oct) costs more than paying the year up front, so check the plan you will use. New learners pay a once-off enrolment fee of R20,000 (not refunded, for building work) and a R750 application fee (not refunded). Gr 12 exam fees are extra: IEB R11,000, or IB R18,500 for IB learners instead. The school says its financial terms and conditions apply.
