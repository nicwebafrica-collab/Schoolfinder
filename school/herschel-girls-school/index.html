About these fees

Independent girls' school in Claremont, Cape Town, Pre-Nursery to Grade 12. 2026 tuition per month: Pre-Nursery R8,890 (R21,580 a term); Nursery R9,035; Reception R9,300; Gr 1-2 R10,665; Gr 3 R14,015; Gr 4-5 R14,455; Gr 6 R14,810; Gr 7 R17,040; Gr 8-11 R18,205; Gr 12 R20,225 (Gr 12 is billed over three terms or 9 instalments). The schedule's annual amounts are inclusive of a 3% discount for payment in full on or before 31 January 2026, which has passed, so no yearly figure is shown. A capital levy of R7,350 a year applies to every grade (R735 a month, or R817 a month over three terms for Gr 12). Extras such as aftercare (Prep only) and electives are not priced on the page.
