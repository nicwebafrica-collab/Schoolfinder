About these fees

Independent school; fees shown are for the youngest group, Gr 0000 (turning 3). The 2027 sheet has a yearly column and a monthly column headed 'INSTALMENTS X 12 (NO DISCOUNT)'; Form 5 pays over 11 months, January to November. Fees differ by grade (see grade list), from R50,620 a year for Gr 0000 to R158,000 for Form 5. A non-refundable enrolment fee of R4,000 is payable on acceptance. Discounts: 5% per annum for payment before 7 January; 10% from the 2nd biological child. Adventure Club (Gr 0000 to Gr 3, optional, paid over ten months Feb to Nov): full time R1,680, three times a week R1,010, half day R840, day visitor R110 per day. Exam fees (Cambridge/IEB) and items such as textbooks, stationery, uniform and excursions are extra; the exam fee lines are hard to match to subjects, so ask the school. A 12-instalment option is also printed; it adds up to more than the yearly fee.
