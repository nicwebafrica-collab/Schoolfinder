About these fees

Independent primary school, Grade 1 to Grade 7. 2027 tuition is R5,340 monthly or R64,080 per year, the same for Grade 1-3 and Grade 4-7. Tuition includes supervision from 06.00 to 7.45, sport, music, arts and culture, and robotics/coding. A non-refundable enrolment fee of R3500 is applicable (the page does not say who pays it; shown as registration). A learning material fee of R2300 is printed as 'once off ... per annum'. Second child gets a 5% discount and a third child 10%. Grade 4-7 also lists 10% off once off annual school fees (paying the year in one payment). Textbooks and stationery, photos, camps, outings and shows, therapy or counselling, professional sport and remedial classes cost extra, no amounts printed. Aftercare is optional: R1 840 per month, R22 080 per year, or R180 day visitor.
