About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. Monthly tuition by grade: Gr R R5 270; Gr 1-3 R6 690; Gr 4 R7 180; Gr 5-6 R7 316; Gr 7 R8 070; Gr 8-9 R8 470; Gr 10 R8 730; Gr 11 R9 140; Gr 12 R11 280. Gr R to 11 pay over 11 months, Gr 12 over 10 months, so no yearly total is shown; the headline is the Gr R monthly fee. Paying the full year upfront gets 5% off (Gr R R55 071.50 up to Gr 12 R107 160.00). Non-refundable application fee: R1 650 once off for South African learners, R4 370 for international learners. The sheet lists no other fees apart from transport. Optional transport by area, from R439 one-way (Ferndale) to R4 053 one-way (Lanseria), round trip R773 to R7 197 a month; ask the school for your area (info@radley.co.za).
