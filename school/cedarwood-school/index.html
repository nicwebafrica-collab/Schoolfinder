About these fees

Independent school; fee sheet covers Gr 1-12 plus pre-vocational and vocational streams. 2027 annual school fee: Gr 1-6 (and Pre-Vocational 4-6) R164 651.03; Gr 7-9 (and Pre-Vocational 7-9) R174 346.22; Gr 10-11 (and Pre-Vocational 10-11, Year 11 & 12 Vocational) R192 653.95; Gr 12 R212 434.47. Pay over 11 months, 1 January to 1 November, by the 1st (Gr 1-6 R14 968.28; Gr 7-9 R15 849.66; Gr 10-11 R17 514.00; Gr 12 R19 312.22), or once by 19 January 2027 with 5% off (Gr 1-6 R156 418.48; Gr 7-9 R165 628.91; Gr 10-11 R183 021.25; Gr 12 R201 812.75). 10% sibling discount. 'Registration' is the non-refundable admissions fee of R25 000.00, paid before a new pupil is enrolled; the sheet does not say if it is once-off or comes off the fees. Fees exclude workbooks, stationery, outings and the school magazine (no amounts printed).
