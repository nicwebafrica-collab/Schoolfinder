About these fees

Christian school, Gr R to Gr 12. 2027 fee schedule, entry grade Gr R shown: R27,093 a year, or 11 monthly payments of R2,463. Other options printed: two half-year payments, four termly payments. Gr 12 pays monthly over 9 months (R4,633) or over 3 terms. Prices include tuition and a computer levy; Gr 10-12 also include a school development levy. Early-payment discounts only if the full amount is paid by the due date: 10% annual (due 1 January), 5% half-yearly, 2.5% termly. Multiple children: 20% off the 3rd child, 30% off the 4th, 50% off the 5th. Application is free. Once-off non-refundable enrolment fee R1,250.
