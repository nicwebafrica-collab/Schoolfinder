About these fees

Fee-paying public high school with boarding (Gibson House). 2027 day fees: Grade 8 R82,025 plus a once-off R5,000 Capital Development Levy (the school gives R87,025 including the levy); Grades 9-12 R80,825. Pay in full, per term (Gr 8 R20,506.25, Gr 9-12 R20,206.25), 10 monthly EFT payments January to October (Gr 8 R8,202.50, Gr 9-12 R8,082.50) or 11 debit orders January to November (Gr 8 R7,456.82, Gr 9-12 R7,347.73). Discounts of 3.5% to 7.5% for paying in full early (by 31 December 2026 or 28 February 2027), as listed on the school's page. 'Deposit' is the R3,500 non-refundable acceptance deposit that secures the place; the school says it is not credited to the fee account. New pupils joining in Grade 9 to 12 pay a smaller once-off levy (R4,000 down to R1,000).
