About these fees

Independent school, Tree Tots (from 18 months) to Gr 4. Fees shown are for Tree Tots: R40,960 a year, or R4,096 a month for 10 months (January to October), or 4 termly payments of R10,240. Fees differ by grade (see grade list). Paying the annual fee by 31 January earns a 2.5% discount (Tree Tots R39,936). Sibling discount: R1,200 a year (R300 a term) on school fees for younger siblings only. The sheet lists once-off sports and stationery levies, compulsory accident insurance and a toiletry levy (see extra costs). Cash is not accepted. The sheet lists no registration or deposit.
