About these fees

Independent school from pre-primary to Gr 12. Each grade has a discounted annual price (Gr 0000 R81,408) that is lower than 12 monthly payments added up, so the monthly figure shown is the normal 12-payment option (Gr 0000 R7,280; Gr 1 R11,390; Gr 8 R15,170). 10-payment options are also printed (Gr 0000 R8,570). Gr 12 has only a 10-payment option, R21,200 a month. The discount is lost if not paid in full by 31 January 2027. New learners pay a R880 application fee and a R13,200 non-refundable enrolment fee. Kidz Club aftercare is optional for pre-primary and preparatory (12-payment prices below). The pre-primary sheet lists an R250 ad-hoc fee without saying how often; ask the school.
