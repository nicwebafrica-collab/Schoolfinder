About these fees

Independent primary school with boarding. 2026 tuition: Grade R R35,970 a year or R3,270 a month for 11 months (January to November), rising to R50,270 a year (R4,570 a month) for Grades 4 to 7. Pre-Primary is R12,870 (1 day), R21,230 (2-3 days) or R29,480 (4-5 days) a year, or R300 a day. A R1,200 non-refundable enrolment fee is paid on acceptance; the sheet doesn't say if it comes off the fees. Boarding (Grades R-7, Monday to Friday) is extra: see the boarding items. The book fee (once-off, Grades R-7) was not yet set on the sheet and is invoiced in Term 1; uniform has no price listed. Ask the school for both.
