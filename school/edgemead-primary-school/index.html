About these fees

Fee-paying public primary school. 2027 school fees per learner: Grades 1-4 R31,920 a year (R3,192 a month, or R7,980 a term); Grades 5-7 R32,190 (R3,219 a month, or R8,048 a term). Grade 1 learners whose deposit was paid pay R30,420 (R3,042 a month, R7,605 a term) because the R1,500 Grade 1 advance payment is deducted. Monthly debit orders run for 10 months, February to November 2027, due on the 1st. Pay in full for a discount on the annual fee: 8% by 31 December 2026 (Grades 1-4 R29,366), 7% by 31 January 2027 (R29,686) or 6% by 28 February 2027 (R30,005); Grades 5-7 R29,615, R29,937 and R30,259. The letter lists no registration fee. Fee relief applications open from 15 February 2027.
