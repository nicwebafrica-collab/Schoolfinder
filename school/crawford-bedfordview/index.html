About these fees

Independent school, Grade 1 to 12 (preparatory and college). 2027 tuition is printed three ways: an annual (discounted) price if paid in full by 31 January 2027, or 12 monthly payments, or 10 monthly payments. The annual price is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so only the 12-month monthly amount is shown. Grade 12 has no 12-month option, only 10 payments. Application fee R880 and non-refundable enrolment fee R8,800 are for new learners, paid through the school's online portal. The preparatory sheet also has an unlabelled Grades 1-7 row; the school does not say what it covers, so ask the school.
