About these fees

Independent primary school in Oak Glen, Bellville. Fees are set by number of children, not by grade; the headline is the first child. 2026 school fees, first child: R4,114 a month (January to December) or R49,368 a year. Two children pay R7,508 a month, three R10,182, four R12,856. The sheet also gives a 5% discount for paying the full 2026 amount before 31 January 2026; that date has passed. Full-day aftercare (until 18:00) for the first child is R1,623 a month; mini aftercare (until 15:15) is R707 a month. No registration or other fees are printed on the sheet, and it lists no grades.
