About these fees

Independent school, Gr 0 to Gr 12. 2026 annual fee for Gr 0 is R113,770 (Gr 1 R118,400, rising to R189,770 in Gr 12; see the by-grade list). Fees are payable annually in advance, termly (three equal instalments at the start of each term) or monthly over ten months (February to November) by debit order; the school does not print the instalment amounts. Paying in full in advance earns a 5% rebate. Fees not paid in full by 31 January 2026 are payable in termly instalments. Application fee R500 per child, non-refundable. Entrance fees payable on acceptance: Gr 0-7 R25,000 (R12,500 refundable and a R12,500 non-refundable development levy), Gr 8-12 R30,000 (R15,000 refundable and R15,000 non-refundable levy). Sibling rebates: 2nd child less 2.5%, 3rd less 7.5%, 4th less 15%, 5th less 20%. A 'B' debenture (R47,000) gives R4,700 off fees a year; Gr 0 with debenture is R109,070.
