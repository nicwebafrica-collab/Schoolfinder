About these fees

Fee-paying public girls' primary school in Queenstown (Komani). 2026 fee structure: R33,561 a year; R8,390.25 a term (first week of each term); R3,051 a month (by the last school day of each month). New pupils pay a R5,000 deposit, which is part of the R33,561, and then R7,140.25 a term or R2,596.50 a month (R2,596 in November) as printed. A R1,000 settlement discount is allowed if fees are settled in full by 31 January 2026, bringing the year to R32,561. This is the 2026 schedule; no 2027 schedule was found. The school's boarding page gives no amounts and describes boarding at Queenstown Girls' High School.
