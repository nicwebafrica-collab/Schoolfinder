About these fees

Fee-paying public boys' school (about 16 km from Waterfall). 2027 fees: R85,500 for new learners (Gr 8), R78,000 for Gr 9-12. The 'deposit' is a non-refundable R7,500 registration deposit for new enrolments. Textbooks are loaned. School warns costs may rise after printing. Gr 8 shown as R78,000 because the school's R85,500 for new boys already includes the R7,500 non-refundable registration deposit (shown separately as the deposit).
