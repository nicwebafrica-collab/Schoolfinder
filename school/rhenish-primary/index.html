About these fees

Public school. 2026 school fees R34,800 a year for Gr 1-7, payable in advance, due 1 January. Payment options: 10 equal EFT instalments from 1 January to 1 October 2026, or ten monthly debit-order instalments January to October (no monthly amount is printed, so monthly is left out). A compulsory ICT levy of R1,500 a year applies to Gr 1-7. A fee of R37,100 is also printed for the pre-primary class; the grade isn't named, so it is left out. Full-payment discounts (5% until 31 January 2026, 2.5% before 28 February 2026) have passed. Exemption forms were due before 27 February 2026.
