About these fees

Independent preparatory school, Gr 1-7, on the same La Lucia campus as Crawford College. Each grade has a discounted annual price (Gr 1 R115,956) that is lower than the monthly payments added up, so the monthly figure shown is the normal 12-payment option (Gr 1 R10,380 a month). A 10-payment option is also printed (Gr 1 R12,210). The annual discount is lost if not paid in full by 31 January 2027, and the account then moves to the 12-month option. The same payment plan must be used for tuition, aftercare and music. New learners pay a R880 application fee and a R5,500 non-refundable enrolment fee, both through the school's online portal. Sibling discounts on tuition: 5% (2nd), 10% (3rd), 15% (4th+). 90 days' notice to leave.
