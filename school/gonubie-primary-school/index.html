About these fees

Public school, Gr R to Gr 7. 2027 school fees per child, monthly over 11 payments: Gr R and Gr 1 R41,030 (R3,730); Gr 2 R36,245 (R3,295); Gr 3-7 R34,430 (R3,130). Headline is Gr R. The notice gives two different payment schedules (20 January to 20 November, and the 1st of each month in advance). Discounts for paying the whole year early (7% by end December 2026, 3% by end February 2027) are conditional and are not shown as the yearly fee. Book and cultural activities fee: Gr R R700 (already paid when the notice was written), Gr 1-7 R600. Beginners packs: Gr R R450, Gr 1 R580. A line saying R650 applies if the book fee is paid only in 2027 is left out because it is unclear. A Gr 7 locker fee is mentioned with no amount. Debit order is preferred at no extra cost.
