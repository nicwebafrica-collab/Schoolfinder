About these fees

Independent school, preschool and Grade R to Grade 11 plus A Level (CAPS and Cambridge). The sheet has no currency symbol on the tuition rows; the levies are printed in rand. Entry level (Preschool and Grade R): annual R14,400, termly R3,600, monthly R1,200. Annual registration of R500 is non-refundable and paid by all students each year. Annual fees are due by 31 January, term fees by the first day of the term, and monthly fees by debit order. Grade 10 and Grade 11 each appear twice on the sheet with different amounts and no explanation, so they are left out; ask the school. Second child 10% and third child or more 15% discount. 'Termly 7%' and 'Annual 10%' are also printed without saying what they apply to. Overdue accounts incur 2.5% interest. The 2026 sheet is the latest; the school was not found to list 2027 fees.
