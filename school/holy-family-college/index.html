About these fees

Independent combined school, Grade RRR to 12, with boarding for Gr 8-12. 2027 day fees depend on grade; Gr RRR is R37,290 a year or R3,390 a month, and Gr 12 is R89,010 a year. Paid in advance by the 1st: Grades 000 to 11 over 11 monthly instalments, Grade 12 over 9. 'Registration' is the R5,000 non-refundable application fee for new learners, plus a R500 administration fee. New boarders pay a R6,500 non-refundable boarding application fee. Boarding (on top of tuition): termly R112,560 a year (R37,520 a quarter), weekly R75,060 a year (R25,020 a quarter). Extra subjects R2,250 a year each; IEB exam fee Gr 11 R4,950 a year; Gr 12 'TBA'. The Gr 10 IEB fee is printed two ways (R3,850 or R3,300 a year); ask the school. Penalties: R100 a month in arrears, R300 per unpaid debit order, a 3% cash deposit fee, one term's fees without written notice, aftercare late charge R100 per 15 minutes after 18h00. The aftercare fee itself isn't printed.
