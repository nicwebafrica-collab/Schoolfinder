About these fees

Independent school, Pre-School (3-6 years) to Gr 12. 2027 fees, monthly in advance over 12 months: Pre-School R4,800; Gr 1-3 R6,400; Gr 4-7 R6,550; Gr 8-11 R6,610. Gr 12 pays R8,196 over 10 months. The annual prices on the sheet (for example R54,720 for Pre-School) are lower than 12 payments added up and apply only if paid up front by 1 January 2027, so no yearly figure is shown. Once-off non-refundable enrolment fee: Pre-School R2,000 (shown as registration), Gr 1-12 R4,000. Included: breakfast, cooked lunch, afternoon snack, academic aftercare, holiday care, textbooks, tablet (Gr 7-12). Not included: stationery and toiletries. Therapy, dance, martial arts, robotics, off-site swimming and outings cost extra (amounts not given). Sibling discount 5% for the 2nd to 4th child. One term's written notice to leave.
