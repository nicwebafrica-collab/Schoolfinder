About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 12. Grade R: R3,520 a month for 12 months (January to December), R42,240 a year, or R40,128 if the year is paid by 31 January (the school calls this a 5% saving). Grade 1-7: R4,050 a month, R48,600 a year, R46,170 if paid by 31 January. Grade 8-9: R5,090 a month, R61,080 a year, R58,026 by 31 January. Grade 10-12: R5,725 a month, R68,700 a year, R65,265 by 31 January. R500 application fee (once-off, non-refundable) and R1,500 enrolment fee (paid in full once the child is accepted); the sheet doesn't say if the enrolment fee comes off the fees. Siblings: 5% off the second child's tuition and 10% off the third and later. Coding and Robotics is compulsory for Grade 1-7: R350 a month for 11 months (January to November), billed with the fees. The sheet mentions an additive fee for licences, workbooks, paper and tissues but prints no amount; ask the school.
