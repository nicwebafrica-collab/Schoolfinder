About these fees

Independent school; the fee sheet covers Gr R to Gr 7 and aftercare. 2027 fees for one child, over 11 months: Gr R R2 850 a month or R31 350 yearly; Gr 1-3 R5 000 or R55 000; Gr 4-7 R5 150 or R56 650. The sheet also prints a 12-month figure (Gr R R2 530, Gr 1-3 R4 580, Gr 4-7 R4 720). Fees include banking fees and are due by the 3rd of each month. Two children in the same phase: Gr R R5 400 (11 months) or R59 400 yearly; Gr 1-3 R9 500 or R104 500; Gr 4-7 R9 785 or R107 635. Each row also has an unlabelled figure (for example R28 575 for one Gr R child); it is not used, so ask the school. Paying in full before the end of January 2027 earns a one-month discount, and sibling discounts apply (no amounts printed). For more than two children, contact the school. A R250 late penalty is added to the account.
