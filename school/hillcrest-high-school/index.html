About these fees

Fee-paying public school. Fees are due on 1 January, or by arrangement in 10 equal payments from January to October. 5% off if paid in full before 31 December 2026, or R1,000 off if paid in full before 28 February 2027. Grade 8: R5,635 a month if the R2,000 enrolment deposit was paid the year before. Learning Support Unit: Gr 8 R64,350, Gr 9 R62,350 (R6,435 and R6,235 a month). Third and later children: 50% off if the first two children's fees are paid in full. A full term's notice is needed before leaving. Fee exemption applications must be finalised by 31 March 2027. Uniform prices are from the A-Team Uniforms July 2026 price list in the school's uniform shop (photo taken on site); the shop says prices can change and are quoted on average sizes.
