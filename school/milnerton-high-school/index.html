About these fees

Public school, Gr 8-12. 2026 school fees are R37,680 a year for Gr 8-12, which includes a compulsory educational consumables fee of R1,140. Gr 8 (entry grade shown): R3,050 a month from March to December (10 payments); the sheet says a R7,180 deposit is taken into account in the Gr 8 annual price, so the deposit is part of the R37,680, not an extra; the monthly payments are what is left after it. Gr 9-12: R3,140 a month from January to December (12 payments). A R1,820 discount applied to annual payments received by 28 February; that date has passed and the discounted annual prices (Gr 8 R28,680, Gr 9-12 R35,860) are not shown as the yearly fee. Art and design levies (R700 each, Gr 10-12) are left out because the sheet does not say how often they are charged. Registration and application fees are not on the sheet. No general office email is published.
