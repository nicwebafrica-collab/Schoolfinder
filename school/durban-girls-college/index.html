About these fees

Independent school, Gr 00 to Gr 12. 2027 tuition per year: Gr 00 R85,100, Gr R R91,200, Gr 1-3 R128,200, Gr 4-7 R148,500, Gr 8-12 R182,000. Monthly invoices run January to October (10 payments). Paying the year in advance by 13 January 2027 is cheaper (Gr 00 R81,270, Gr R R87,100, Gr 1-3 R122,430, Gr 4-7 R141,820, Gr 8-12 R173,810). 'Registration' is the R950 once-off, non-refundable application fee. 'Deposit' is the once-off non-refundable acceptance fee, which rises with grade (Gr 00 R10,638, Gr R R11,400, Gr 1-3 R16,025, Gr 4-7 R18,563, Gr 8-12 R22,750). Every child also pays R200 a year for accident insurance and Netcare 911. Compulsory levies: capital R4,400 and facility R2,300 a year. Aftercare R2,040 a month full-time, R1,638 or R1,374 part-time (10 months), R117 a day casual.
