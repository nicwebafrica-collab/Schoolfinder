About these fees

Independent school from Grade 00 to Grade 12. 2026 tuition per year: Gr 00 R79,900, Gr R R85,600, Gr 1-3 R120,400, Gr 4-7 R139,400, Gr 8-12 R170,900. On top of tuition every child pays a development levy of R1,550 a term and R185 a year for accident insurance and Netcare 911. 'Deposit' is the once-off acceptance fee, which rises with the grade (R9,988 for Gr 00 up to R21,363 for Gr 8-12). Gr 12 pays a R9,669 IEB fee. Weekly boarding for Gr 8-12 is R139,400 a year. The development levy is charged every term. The acceptance fee is non-refundable. The school's 2026 fee schedule gives cheaper totals for paying tuition in full by 14 Jan 2026 (e.g. Gr 00 R76,300, Gr 8-12 R163,210), termly payments (Gr 00 R19,975, Gr 8-12 R42,725 a term), or an estimated monthly debit order from January to October that includes other charges (e.g. Gr 00 about R8,800, Gr 8-11 about R17,950, Gr 12 about R18,900).
