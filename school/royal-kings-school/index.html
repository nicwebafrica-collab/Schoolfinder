About these fees

Independent school, RRR to Grade 12. 2027 fees, paid in advance monthly by the 1st or quarterly at the start of each term. RRR to Gr R: R2,500 a month, R7,500 a quarter, R30,000 a year. Gr 1-3: R3,000 / R9,000 / R36,000. Gr 4-6: R3,300 / R9,900 / R39,600. Gr 7-9: R3,500 / R10,500 / R42,000. Gr 10-11: R3,600 / R10,800 / R43,200. Gr 12: R11,000 a quarter and R44,000 a year; the sheet's monthly figure of R4,000 for Gr 12 does not fit the yearly figure, so ask the school. Paying the year in full by 28 February 2027 gives 5% off. New learners pay a R1,000 registration fee and a R650 administration fee; current learners pay R800 re-registration. Aftercare and swimming lessons (January to April and September to December) are listed at R1,000 and R800 a month; we couldn't confirm which amount is which, so ask the school. Sibling rebates: 3%, 5% and 7%.
