About these fees

Independent school (Gauteng campus, Kempton Park), Grade RRR to Grade 7. 2027 fees, monthly or as a yearly amount: Grade RRR-RR half day R3,190 a month (R38,280 a year), full day R3,960 (R47,520); Grade R-3 R3,740 (R44,880); Grade 4-6 R3,795 (R45,540); Grade 7 R3,905 (R46,860). New learners pay a non-refundable registration fee (R750 for RRR-RR, R1,000 for Grade R-7), a R500 assessment fee (Grade R-7) and a R2,000 non-refundable deposit. Aftercare R1,100 a month, optional (the sheet's yearly amounts with aftercare are R11,000 more, so it looks like 10 months; ask the school). Fees are payable in advance by the 1st. Late fee R100 a day. Third child 10% off tuition.
