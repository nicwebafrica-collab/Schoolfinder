About these fees

Independent remedial (learning disability) school. 2027 fee structure, same for all grades. Two payment plans: annual fee R132 500 paid before the first day of the 2027 school year, or 10 monthly payments of R15 063, or 12 monthly payments of R13 833 (so no single monthly figure is shown). A lower annual price of R125 875 applied if paid by 16 December 2026 (a discount price). 10% sibling discount. Annual levy, due before the first payment of 2027: Gr 1-3 R5 270, Gr 4-7 R5 535. Once-off enrolment fee R25 000, 50% refundable when leaving. Aftercare is optional at R1 755 a month. The sheet is a text PDF read twice; the 2026 column is not used.
