About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to 9 (the DBE masterlist lists it as a primary school; the fee page also shows Gr 8-9). 'Fee Structure 2027', payment over 12 months, January to December. Grade R: R7,410 a term, R2,600 a month by debit order, or R29,640 a year up front. Gr 1-3: R8,270 a term, R2,900 a month, R33,060 a year. Gr 4-7: R9,690 a term, R3,100 a month, R35,340 a year. Gr 8-9: R9,410 a term, R3,300 a month, R37,620 a year. A once-off R2,500 registration and admin fee applies. The annual option qualifies for a discount if paid by 1 January 2027; the page doesn't say how much. Optional: Grade 1-7 aftercare (R850 registration and admin, R4,800 a term, R1,200 a month or R13,680 a year), holiday programme R100 a day, Saturday or weekday tutor (R4,000 a term, R1,100 a month or R10,000 for 10 months), day visit or ad hoc attendance R100 a day. The school lists two campuses (New Quay and Voortrekker).
