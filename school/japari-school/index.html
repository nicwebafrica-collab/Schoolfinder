About these fees

Independent school, Grade R to Grade 7 (listed by the education department as a special needs centre). 2027 tuition for Grade R: R10,000 a month over 11 months (January to November), or R110,000 a year. Gr 1-3 R17,040 a month / R187,440 a year; Gr 4-5 R16,280 / R179,080; Gr 6-7 R15,940 / R175,340. From 2027, Grades 1 to 5 get group therapy and individual speech and occupational therapy; Grades 6 and 7 get support where needed. 'Registration' is the non-refundable enrolment fee, due on accepting a place: Grade R R2,000, Grades 1 to 7 R15,000. Grades 1 to 7 also pay a R10,000 'Deposit', refunded when the child leaves. Early settlement discounts on the annual fee, Grades 1 to 7 only: 10% off before 15 November 2026 (saves Gr 1-3 R18,744; Gr 4-5 R17,908; Gr 6-7 R17,534); 8% off before 15 December 2026 (saves R14,995; R14,326; R14,027); 5% off before 31 January 2027 (saves R9,372; R8,954; R8,767). Stationery packs are invoiced in January 2027 (no amount).
