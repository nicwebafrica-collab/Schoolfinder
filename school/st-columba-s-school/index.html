About these fees

Independent school, Gr 0000 (from 18 months) to Gr 10. 2026 fees per year (debit order over 12 months): Gr 0000 R38,096 (R3,175); Gr 000 R36,408 (R3,034); Gr 00 R43,689 (R3,641); Gr 0 R50,971 (R4,248); Gr 1-6 R77,913 (R6,493); Gr 7 R84,405 (R7,034); Gr 8-9 R89,602 (R7,467); Gr 10 R96,546 (R8,045). Monthly amounts are rounded, so they differ slightly from the yearly fee. Amounts are for the first child; sibling discount on annual fees is 5% for a 2nd child, 7% for a 3rd, 9% for a 4th. Capital development levy R2,160 a year per family. Admission fee R5,000 to secure a place, once per family; application fee R400. Aftercare R1,820 a month (Gr 000 to 10). The 5% pay-upfront discount was due by 31 January and has passed.
