About these fees

Independent school, pre-primary (Gr 0000) to Grade 12. 2027 tuition is printed three ways: an annual (discounted) price if paid in full by 31 January 2027, or 12 monthly payments, or 10 monthly payments. The annual price is lower than the 12 monthly payments added up, so only the 12-month monthly amount is shown. Grade 12 has no 12-month option, only 10 payments. Application fee R880 and non-refundable enrolment fee R8,800 are for new learners, paid through the school's online portal. Aftercare is optional: Kidz Club full day R25,320 for the year (pre-primary), preparatory full day R26,844 and half day R21,252 for the year, all discounted annual prices. The pre-primary half-day row is unreadable and the ad hoc aftercare fee of R185 has no stated frequency; ask the school.
