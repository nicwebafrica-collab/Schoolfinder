About these fees

Independent high school (Abbotts College, ADvTECH); the same 2027 tuition applies at all Abbotts campuses. Headline is Grade 8: R77,700 a year or 10 monthly payments of R7,770; a 12-month debit order (R6,800) is also printed, not for Grade 12. Paying the year by 31 January 2027 saves 5%. Registration fee R350 and enrolment fee R2,650 (new students) are once-off and non-refundable. Grade 12 part-time is R17,540 per subject. Student card R50. Locker R300 optional (Gr 9-12); lockable cell phone pouch R320 compulsory in Gr 8-9. Grade 10-12 subject fees vary by subject and are billed extra. Sibling discounts on tuition: 5% (2nd), 10% (3rd), 15% (4th+).
