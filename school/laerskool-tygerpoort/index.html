About these fees

Public school (Afrikaans name; fee sheet in Afrikaans). 2027 school fees for Gr RR and R: R38,445 a year including a non-refundable first payment of R2,200 due by 31 December 2026, then 11 monthly payments of R3,295 (January to November); the first payment is part of the total. Gr 1-7: R36,520 a year, first payment R2,200, then 11 x R3,120. Pay in full by 31 December 2026 for 10% off (Gr RR and R R34,600; Gr 1-7 R32,865), or by 28 February 2027 for 7.5% off (R35,560; R33,780). Optional aftercare: Gr RR and R R16,625 a year, Gr 1-7 R19,100 a year, both including a R400 non-refundable registration fee, or R1,475 / R1,700 a month for 11 months.
