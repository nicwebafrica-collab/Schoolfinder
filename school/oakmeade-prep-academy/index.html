About these fees

Independent school with pre-primary groups (RRRR to RR, R3,200 a month), Grade R to Grade 12. 2026 monthly fees (12 instalments, January to December, on or before the 1st): Grade R R4,980; Grades 1-3 R5,140; Grades 4-6 R6,160; Grades 7-9 R6,590; Grades 10-11 R7,400; Grade 12 R7,670 (includes IEB exam fees). Sibling monthly rates are lower (for example Grade R R4,230). A discounted annual fee applies only if paid in full by 31 January 2026 (Grade R R54,780; Grades 1-3 R56,540; Grades 4-6 R67,760; Grades 7-9 R72,490; Grades 10-11 R81,400). Application fee R300 per child, non-refundable, with the application form. Enrolment fee R3,000 per child, once-off, non-refundable, within 30 days of acceptance. Fee schedule is for 2026.
