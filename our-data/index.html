What's in the data

For each school with checked fees, we hold:

  • Yearly school fees by grade or phase, and the year they apply to
  • Other costs where the school publishes them, such as registration, levies, uniforms, books, camps and outings
  • A monthly estimate (yearly cost divided by 12, clearly labelled)
  • The source document and the date we last checked it
  • Fee history, kept year by year, so changes over time can be seen

As of 13 Nov 2026, 183 schools have fully checked fees, across KwaZulu-Natal, Gauteng, Western Cape and Eastern Cape. We add more every week.

How we keep it right

FIND

The school's own document

Every fee comes from the school's own published fee schedule or letter to parents. We don't estimate fees from averages or ranges.

CHECK

A person checks every amount

Each number is compared with the school's document before it goes live. Nothing is published automatically.

UPDATE

New year, new check

When a school publishes next year's fees, we check them and keep the old year's fees in the history.

Our accuracy record

  • Every school page shows its source and a “checked on” date.
  • In a random re-check of live schools on 13 Nov 2026, every number we could read matched the school's own document.
  • If we can't find a school's own document, we show “fees not checked yet” instead of a guess.
  • Parents can report a wrong price at any time, and we check every report within a week.

Independent, always

  • Schools can't pay to change their numbers or their place in any list.
  • Anything paid on SchoolMap is clearly labelled.
  • Parents always use SchoolMap for free.
  • Working with an organisation on data never changes what parents see.

Personal information

The data is about schools, not people. We don't hold or share personal details of parents, learners or staff, and we don't sell personal information.

For organisations

We're open to talking with organisations that could use checked school cost data, for example in financial planning, education research, insurance, banking or media.

If that's you, email info@schoolmap.co.za with your organisation's name and how you'd like to use the data. We'll get back to you.
